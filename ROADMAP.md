# Roadmap

This is a living document. It reflects the current best thinking
on what to build next — not a contract. Things change as you learn
more about your users and your product. Update it freely.

## Shipped

113 versions shipped. Last shipped: **five queued improvements in one release, most of them about the phone (v0.5.81)** -- the Breeding Codes matrix gains an opt-in **Pin code labels** control that keeps the row of code headings visible while you scroll a long species list, so a circle's column stays identifiable instead of becoming a guess; it is off by default, the choice is per-session, and it works in the "↔ Unbounded" view, so pressing it from the normal view takes you there and pins in one press and pressing it again puts you back. The Settings **Sharing** preference becomes three independent switches, for the coordinates, the Google Maps link, and the Apple Maps link, so any combination is available instead of the previous two; switch all three off and the share popup says so plainly rather than offering a button that would copy nothing, and if you had chosen coordinates only, that is still exactly what you get. Three fixes round it out: the iPhone's Dynamic Island no longer covers the map sub-tabs in expanded map mode or the in-app Help header (and the Help table of contents no longer runs past the bottom of the panel on an iPad, which had made its last few entries unreachable); and in the filter rows on Life List, Multimedia, Checklists, Breeding Codes, Species Detail, and Calendar, the dropdowns and date fields no longer render larger than the buttons beside them -- a mismatch that used to invert at large text sizes, leaving the dropdowns small for exactly the person who enlarged their text. A "copy the closest street address" option for the share pin was considered and deliberately not built, so the pin still assembles everything on your own device and works with no connection.

Previously: **share a map location by dropping a pin (v0.5.80)** -- right-click, or long-press on a touch screen, anywhere on a birding map to drop a share pin, then press once to copy that exact spot as a share-ready block: the GPS coordinates plus tappable Google Maps and Apple Maps links, ready to paste into a text message. It works on the Map Explorer's sightings view, Species Detail, Statistics, and the Named Birds card maps; on the Map Explorer's Hotspots, Nearby Lifers, and Media Targets views, where that gesture already drops the search-center pin, that existing pin simply gains the copy action instead of competing with a second one. A new Settings "Sharing" preference chooses between coordinates with map links (the default) and coordinates only, showing a live example of exactly what will be copied, and a corner map tool plants the pin at the view center so the whole feature is reachable without a pointer gesture. No URL shortener and no lookup of any kind -- the links are built on the device from coordinates it already has, so the location never leaves it and the feature works with no connection.

Earlier: **Macaulay Library media no longer shows a "Missing feature Cookies" error (v0.5.76)** -- the Cornell Lab put a bot check in front of the Macaulay Library, and that check cannot run inside an embedded player, so every photo, recording, and video embedded in SnowRaven turned into Cornell's error card. Rendering the media directly from Cornell's servers would have restored inline playback and was deliberately not done: it would route around protection they had just deployed. When the check is lifted, players return on their own.

## On the Horizon

Ideas worth tracking, not yet scheduled:

- **Add the iOS focus-zoom guard to the Map Explorer's form controls** -- its nine `<select>` and input controls carry no `.sr-input-16` at all, a miss from the v0.5.61 sweep, so tapping any of them zooms the page on iOS. It shows no size mismatch today because the sidebar is internally consistent at 0.75rem, which is why it went unnoticed; adding the guard was only safe once v0.5.81's shared `max(16px, 0.75rem)` rule existed, and now it does. A real defect, found while measuring that fix and deliberately left out of it.
- **Close the remaining 320px/200% horizontal-scroll leaks** -- the count-and-view cluster leak is now FIXED on both affected tabs (`count-cluster-scroll-leak`), and this entry's earlier form was wrong on three counts, all corrected by the Evaluator's browser measurements: it is ONE tab, not two (`LifeList.tsx` renders only the Multimedia tab; its panel id `life-list` is legacy), the 3px is not a constant but tracks the count label's text (`428 species` reproduces it exactly, `1247 species` gives 12px, `88 species` gives none), and Breeding Codes did **not** avoid the leak -- it carried `.sr-wrap-flex` and was the single largest unclipped overflower on any tab at that size (475px cluster in a 296px box, 179px past the viewport), because `flexShrink: 0` held it at max-content so the computed `flex-wrap: wrap` was inert. What remains tracked here are the separate, unrelated leaks measured at the same time and deliberately scoped out: Statistics 60px (a `.sr-favicon` image), Checklists 42px, Calendar 29px, and Breeding Codes' own residual 31px from its filter pills. None share the fixed cause.
- **Give `.sr-skip-link` its safe-area inset** -- the "Skip to main content" link is a third `position: fixed` surface with the same escape v0.5.81 fixed on the map fullscreen panel and the Help overlay, focusing to 16px from the *physical* top, inside the Dynamic Island band. It is the most direct of the three, being an interactive control rather than a title, and it is a one-rule change: its positioning already lives in `globals.css`, so it needs the `.sr-ios-app`-gated variant and nothing lifted.
- **Confirm the share-pin long-press on a real touch device (Species Detail and Named Birds)** -- those two maps mount with `cooperativeGestures`, which intercepts one-finger touch. A stationary long-press should be unaffected, but that cannot be proven in jsdom and has not met a phone. Both surfaces already ship with desktop right-click and the keyboard map tool, so the feature is reachable either way; if the long-press does not land, the pre-approved fallback is passive listeners on the map's canvas container, confined to `useMapLongPressDrop` (and `README.md` plus `website/index.html`, which say "anywhere on a birding map", would need correcting in the same edit).
- **Confirm native pinch-to-zoom on the Breeding Codes matrix on a real iPhone** — the mobile matrix relies on native viewport pinch to magnify the dot-width code columns (no in-app zoom control). The narrowing itself is the core win and works regardless of pinch, but the pinch-to-magnify behavior should get an eyeball on a real device; iOS 0.5.73 build 1 is on TestFlight for exactly this check (it includes the v0.5.70 Unbounded-view fix, so both Normal and Unbounded narrow on-device). If pinch is unsatisfying on-device, the Designer reserved a `−/Fit/+` fallback control (iOS-only, one-line flip) as the escape hatch.
- **Route the remaining `Math.round` percent sites through `fmtSharePct`** -- v0.5.79 fixed the observer-count legend so a nonzero share never renders as a rounded "0%", but pre-existing whole-percent displays elsewhere (e.g. that chart's "% solo" caption) still use bare `Math.round` and carry the same latent zero-collapse for tiny nonzero shares; a small sweep would route them through the shared helper (flagged by the Tester during the v0.5.79 run).
- **Name the metric in the Calendar's day accessible name** -- a data day currently announces a bare numeral, which is ambiguous under the metric toggle (species / checklists / total individuals all read the same to a screen reader). Adding the metric noun to the accessible name resolves it.
- **Version the persisted map-style blob** -- the cached tuned basemap style is stored with no schema version, so a future style change has no way to invalidate a stale saved copy.
- **Escape `namedBirdKey`'s `::` separator** -- the name-plus-species key joins on a raw `::`, so a bird name containing that sequence could in principle collide with a different pair. No known real collision; a bounded correctness tidy, and the key-parity test would have to move in lockstep.
- **Richer Calendar day popups / county-style shading parity** — the Calendar day popup now lists a day's checklists with each row's time, location, and species count (v0.5.63), but still not the day's *species*; a natural parallel would extend the map's county-style shading affordances (e.g. a per-species calendar, or listing the day's species names in the popup). Not requested — noted as an obvious follow-on to the shipped tab.
- **County shading on the Species Detail and Statistics maps** — the breeding atlas already appears on those maps, so extending the county overlay to them is a natural parallel (deferred out of the v0.5.46 county feature; it would now carry all three metrics, including v0.5.54's Completeness).
- **Completeness targets ranked by findability** — upgrade the county-completeness targets list from its v0.5.54 taxonomic-order floor to a "which could I actually chase" ranking. The public eBird API has no all-time frequency product and the recency-based alternative doubles the per-county calls, so this needs a data-source decision; the `completenessTargets` seam accepts a ranked pool without a route change.
- **Non-US county support** — v0.5.46 draws US counties only (public-domain Census/TIGER geometry); other countries' eBird Subnational2 regions would need different boundary sources and naming.
- **Per-species county choropleth** — "shade the counties where I've seen Acorn Woodpecker" — a larger, different feature than the all-species county life-list shading.
