Today the inbox sheet's footer has one button, Clear, and the New marks go only when you close the sheet. Proposed adds Mark read beside it. Press it: every New mark goes, the badge drops to zero, nothing is removed, and the button rests until a new alert arrives. Every frame below is live. Press Mark read, close with the X, reopen from the bell, use Clear, or add an alert with the button under the device.

What this direction decides

  • Label: "Mark read", your words. Clear already acts on the whole inbox without saying "all", so the pair reads as two whole-inbox actions. "Mark all read" is in the Label switch above so you can compare how both wrap at 320 and 200%.
  • Place: just before Clear, in Clear's own quiet register, so the one action that removes things keeps the trailing edge. The two buttons move as a pair: under the fine print when the line is short, side by side when it fits.
  • Rest state: the button is disabled whenever nothing in the open sheet is New, the same way Clear is disabled when the inbox is empty. No confirmation, because nothing is removed.
  • Focus: the moment you press it the button goes quiet, so focus steps to the Close button at the top of the sheet, the same place the Clear confirmation sends it after a clear. Never to nowhere.
  • What a screen reader hears: one polite sentence, "Marked read." It names the action and never a count.
  • The marks: the green dot and the word New fade out in place over 160 ms, then go. With Reduce Motion on they simply go. The Marks switch above shows the instant version for comparison.
