# big-old-life-lists: held copy (for the Deployer and the user's yes)

Nothing here is written to a published surface by this run. The CHANGELOG
paragraph goes in with the Deployer's version bump; the What's New sentence is
App Store copy and waits for the user's explicit yes.

## (a) CHANGELOG.md paragraph (proposed)

SnowRaven holds a large, old eBird life list with less memory: each loaded copy
of your observations now takes about a third of the memory it did, so the app
accepts a bigger eBird backup on every platform: 64 MB on the Mac, iPhone, and
iPad; 80 MB on Android; and 100 MB on Windows and on web or a Pi (it was 50 MB
everywhere). A backup over the limit is turned away the moment you choose it,
before it is read, with the limit named under the row. The Macaulay Library
export keeps its 50 MB limit. Life list milestones now run past 3,000, every 500
species up to 10,000. Nothing else on screen changes.

## (b) App Store What's New sentence (proposed, HELD for the user's yes)

Larger eBird backups now load on iPhone and iPad (up to 64 MB), using less
memory, and life list milestones continue past 3,000 species to 10,000.

## (c) Not proposed

No website, README, F-Droid listing or privacy policy sentence names any of
these numbers, and none is proposed. The F-Droid changelog files for the release
are the Deployer's, written from (a) when the user approves them.
