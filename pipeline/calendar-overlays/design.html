Specimen 1

The Calendar tab with the rich overlays on

Same tab, same controls, same shading and popup. With an overlay on, the month cards reflow a little wider and every birded day becomes a tile: the count on top, then one row per fact. Media rows are the Multimedia tab's camera, microphone and video glyphs, each with that day's count of Macaulay Library items in that format (with the ML export not loaded, one generic media glyph with the total; try the bar). Breeding rows are the actual eBird codes recorded that day, strongest first, each led by its category circle (solid Confirmed, half Probable, open Possible) and followed by the number of species carrying that code; past three codes the tile says "+N" and the popup has the rest. The Every code / By category control beside the Breeding switch chooses between that and one row per category: the circle, the category name (Conf, Prob, Poss) and the number of species with evidence at that category, no codes at all. Press By category and look at June 21: "FY 1 / CF 1 / NB 1 / +2" becomes "Conf 3 / Poss 2". It dims when Breeding is off and is remembered with the switches. Look at May 17, June 21 and July 4 first, then flip both switches off: today's calendar, byte for byte. Use Textures, All years and the species filter (Oak Titmouse) all work.

Specimen 2

One tile, up close: the two forms it takes

Each tile picks its form by its own width, not the window's. Above about 38px it is the rich form: glyph, code and count in three aligned columns. Below that (a phone cell at 100%, or any cell at 200% text) it is the condensed form: one media glyph with the total, the strongest code with its circle, and "+N" for the rest; on the narrowest cells the glyph and its number simply stack. Both forms are in the tile at once and the stylesheet shows one, so a resize never re-renders.

Specimen 3

Over every shade, solid and textured

Each row is one combination of facts across the five tiers and the zero cell. The rows are drawn in the cell's own number color, so the AA guard that already holds the number on every tier holds them too. With Use Textures on, the fact block takes the same tier-color backing the count already wears over the hatch. Switch the theme in the bar to see dark.

Specimen 4

The day popup with both overlays on

Saturday, May 17, 2025 at Point Reyes. The header carries the day's facts in full: per-format media totals and every code with its category. Each checklist row lists its own media by format and every code it recorded as a chip in the Breeding Codes tab's tier colors: code, label, category, and how many species carried it on that checklist. A checklist with neither shows nothing extra. With both overlays off the popup is today's, byte for byte.

Specimen 5

Large view: the overview keeps a condensed form

The Large thumbnails are the whole-year glance, so they keep round 1's quiet marks: a media square in the bottom-left corner, a category circle (solid, half, open) in the bottom-right, sized with the card and hidden with the day number under the 152px floor. The counts and codes live in Compact view and the popup.

Specimen 6

Phone, 320px wide

The phone tier as shipped: cells cap to their 32px track and keep the 44px touch height. At 100% the cells are just narrow enough to take the condensed form; at 200% (use the bar) each glyph and its number stack and the tiles grow taller, while nothing widens past the viewport.
