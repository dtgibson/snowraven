Year overview

The View → Year density: all twelve months as small heatmap thumbnails in a 3×4 grid, like the back of a wall calendar. Day numbers drop away — the shading is the whole signal, so a year's rhythm (dark spring migration, thin mid-summer, the December spike) reads in one glance. The metric, year navigation, textures, and legend all still apply. Hovering a thumbnail reveals an Open → affordance; clicking it drops back into the big Months view scrolled to that month, and clicking an individual day opens the same day popup.
