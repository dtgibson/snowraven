Named Birds card at phone widths and large text

The open card as it ships today beside the proposed fix, at each configuration the brief names, for a typical named bird and for a 120-character name with no spaces. Every "Proposed" frame runs the real stylesheet rules written for the Engineer to lift. Players are stand-ins; nothing loads from the network.

What changes, in four lines.
  • Sighting row. When date, place and checklist link cannot share a line, the checklist link takes a line of its own under the date and the place keeps the first line. Date first, nothing clipped, the link alone on its line and 44px tall on a phone. Decided by the row's own width in em, so it also catches a desktop window at 200% text; desktop at 100% is unchanged.
  • Tile meta row. Nothing in it stays nowrap, separators ride with the item before them, and the gallery drops to two columns, then one, as the section narrows in em. Three columns everywhere desktop shows three today.
  • Header. "Media of {name}" and "Where {name} has been seen" wrap a long spaceless name inside the card; the glyph stays on the first line.
  • Header date range (new, measured here). The collapsed header's "Mar 2, 2025 – Feb 10, 2026" line is end-aligned and nowrap, so at 320px and 200% text it runs 30px past the card's left edge and the first date is cut. On a phone it may break at a space, right-aligned.
  • Tier-3 badge (light). The species name's hover and focus color becomes #236A41, 5.20:1 and 4.75:1 on the tile's two stops, up from 4.54:1 and 4.14:1. Swatches at the bottom.

Tier-3 milestone badge, species name on hover and focus

Light tier 3 is the one tile where the shared hover color, --sr-accent, falls under 4.5:1 on a gradient stop. The fix is one badge-local property that only tier 3 reads differently. Resting colors, every other tier and dark mode are untouched; the dark swatch is here to show that.

Measured, not assumed

The "Today" frames reproduce the built app's numbers: at 320px and 200% text the place box collapses to 0px wide and the checklist link runs 109px past the card; at 641px and 200% the three 137px tiles put "Macaulay Library" 49.5px past the tile edge; the 120-character header runs 1,595px past the card. The "Proposed" frames were measured in Chromium and WebKit at every configuration on this page: no text ink, link or icon outside the card or its tile, and no card scroll. The timeline strip and the real players are omitted; neither changes.
