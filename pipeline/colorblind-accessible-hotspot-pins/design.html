The tier ring

Behind a small switch, every ramp pin gains a thin ring just inside its rim, split into five fixed segments. A pin's tier fills that many segments, clockwise from the top; the rest stays as a faint track so you can see how much is left. Nothing new is colored: the ring is the same white as the glyph the pins already carry.

1

Filled segments count the tier. One segment is tier 1, a closed ring is tier 5. From across the map you read extent, like a clock face; up close you can count.

2

The faint track completes the circle. It shows the denominator, so three segments read as three of five, not just "some arc".

3

The glyph still carries the kind. Check for visited, two dots for unvisited, exactly as shipped. The ring lives in the empty band between glyph and rim, so the two never touch.

This pin is tier 3, enlarged. The map, legend, and popup all draw the ring from one shared geometry, so they can never drift apart.

Off by default, yours with one switch

The ring is opt-in, exactly like Use Textures on the county and atlas shading: a small switch in the Color pins by block, off by default, and your choice is remembered between sessions. Try it here; the strip follows the switch.
