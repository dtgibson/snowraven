    When eBird isn't reachable

    The three standard states, spoken in the app's usual voice. Your own data — the X count and “Recently added” — always still renders.

    No eBird key

    “eBird API key not configured. Add it in Settings.” No fetches are attempted. Counties you fetched before still shade from cache; everything else stays a plain outline.

    Shown: shade control · popup · counties in view

    You're offline

    Cached counties keep their shading, and “Recently added” still works — it comes from your own backup. Clicking an uncached county says so plainly: no spinner, no blank popup.

    Shown: popup · counties in view

    eBird error

    “eBird didn't respond.” Already-shaded counties keep their shading; the failed county stays plain. Click it again to retry — no reload needed, and errors are never cached.

    Shown: popup · counties in view
