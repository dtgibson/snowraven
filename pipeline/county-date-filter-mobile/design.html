What changes, in plain words

One block, its own row

On phones the county picker and the date range drop out of the pill cloud onto a row of their own: county, then From, then To, all full width and all the same height as the pills beside them.

Empty fields say what they are

The word From or To sits inside each date field. It turns green with the field once a date is chosen. The lone arrow goes; the words do its job.

No dangling lines

The thin separators between pill groups are hidden on phones, on Multimedia's whole row and on Checklists' two rows. On one line they help; in a wrapped cloud they land at line ends and look broken.

What stays the same

The pickers are still the phone's own county and date pickers. Filtering, results, desktop and iPad are untouched.
