## County and date filters on small screens (county-date-filter-mobile)

### What this does
On phones (640px and narrower) the county picker and the date range on Multimedia, Breeding Codes, Species Detail, Checklists and the Map Explorer sidebar become one aligned block: the county picker full width on top, then a joined From / To pair whose fields say "From" and "To" inside them, so an empty field is identifiable even on iOS, which paints an empty date input blank. The narrow three-row tower (blank box, lone arrow line, blank box, with the county centred against it), the dangling pill-row separators and the five different control heights are gone. Desktop and iPad render as before, and what the filters do is unchanged.

### How to test
1. Build and serve the app against the synthetic demo dataset (`website/tools/demo-data`), then open it at 390px wide (or on an iPhone).
2. Multimedia: under the switches, "All Counties" spans the row; beneath it a joined pair reads "From" and "To" with no arrow between them and no separators anywhere in the pill row.
3. Pick a county and a From date: the county row, the From field and its word turn green; the To field stays neutral. Clear them and everything returns to neutral.
4. Repeat on Breeding Codes, Species Detail (Clear filter appears on its own line under the pair once a filter is set), Checklists ("Where & when": county, pair, then the count right-aligned on its own line) and Map Explorer (open Filters: the Date Range pair now joins, has the words and tints when set).
5. Set the in-app Text size to 200% at 320px wide: nothing scrolls sideways. In Chrome the date value drops beneath its word rather than being cut off; in Safari it stays beside the word except on Checklists.
6. Widen the window past 640px: every tab's filters look exactly as they did before.

### Notes for reviewer
- **One deliberate deviation from the approved CSS, forced by measurement (decisions.md E2).** The spec put the word absolutely inside a fixed 3.75em input padding. Built that way, the date value was clipped at 320px / 200% text on Checklists (both engines) and the Map sidebar (Chromium), because the width a word-plus-date line needs depends on the engine and the locale's date format. On a phone the field now draws the box, the word is in flow, and the field wraps the value beneath the word only when the two cannot share a line. The approved look is unchanged wherever it fits, which includes every iOS configuration measured. Two follow-on details are load-bearing and each has a guard: the pair is a block rather than a flex column (WebKit otherwise sized fields at 55px instead of 31px), and the input carries its field's background rather than `transparent` (WebKit otherwise paints an empty date as today's date in full text colour).
- **Desktop mechanism.** The five hand-drawn desktop drawings moved into `globals.css` verbatim, as five named registers under `:where()` (zero specificity), so the phone tier overrides them without `!important`. `lib/dateRangeFieldsCss.test.ts` resolves the real cascade in jsdom (importance, specificity, source order, inline styles, width tiers) and asserts the desktop values equal the pre-change inline styles and the phone values equal the unified register.
- **Guard changes, all deliberate:** `.sr-ctl-label` roster 22 -> 24; Map Explorer native `sr-input-16` count 8 -> 6 with the two dates counted on the shared pair (nine total unchanged); `scrollLeakCss` scans the new block; `filterControlSizeCss` asserts no positive `min-width` on the block and that the global `.sr-field-row` stacking stays at <=480; `MapExplorerInputZoom` asserts the visible words point at the named inputs. `mapFabCascade.test.ts` had a real analyzer bug (it scored a zero-specificity `:where()` selector containing an attribute as (0,2,0)); it is fixed with a depth-aware split, verified to change no other selector's reading across 4,946 selectors, and `sr-whenwhere` joined its named ancestor resolutions.
- **QA fix (decisions.md E13).** WebKit ignores `min-height` on a natively drawn select, so Checklists' county (the one county select without `appearance: none`) rendered 23px on desktop WebKit and 29px on iOS. The shared phone-tier county rule now removes the native control (the other three already did inline), and Checklists' county draws the same `▾` caret as the other three on phones only; desktop keeps its native select, pixel-identical to HEAD.
- **Breeding Codes has two separators**, not one as the design recorded; both are lifted and hidden on phones, which matches the approved mockup (decisions.md E6). Multimedia has six, not five.
- **Accessibility:** accessible names stay "County", "From date", "To date"; the arrow is now `aria-hidden` everywhere (VoiceOver no longer reads "right arrow"); the visible word is a `<label for>` on a `useId()` id, `aria-hidden` so it is not read twice; the focus ring on a phone is drawn on the field the user sees.
- **Verified:** build and eslint clean; full frontend suite 7,427 passed / 3 skipped; 35 of 35 guard mutations red and 6 equivalent rewrites green. Real renders: 120 phone configurations (Chromium and WebKit x 320/390/402px x 100%/200% x light/dark x five surfaces, each at rest and set), zero failures, with the same probe failing HEAD everywhere; iOS Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, Mobile Safari) on all five surfaces; desktop A/B against HEAD at 1280/800/641px x 100%/200% in both engines: every control's geometry and computed style identical, WebKit pixel-identical on all 60 states, Chromium within its own same-build raster noise.
- **Known limitations.** Chromium snaps the 1.5px border to 1px, so its join overlaps by half a pixel (WebKit exact). Desktop WebKit reports the Checklists county select's computed `display` as `inline-block` under its transparent wrapper, with identical geometry and pixels. iOS was checked at 402pt only (no supported iPhone is 320pt wide).
- **Release consequence (for the ship, not this change):** `appstore/screenshots/iphone-6.9/05-species-detail.png` photographs the old tower and needs a recapture plus a store-record update at the ship that carries this.
