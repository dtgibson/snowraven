# Change Brief: eBird request economy

## What is changing
Four saved ideas from the 1.0.64 audit, plus the measured answers to the user's two questions. (1) The Statistics escapee check's checklist lookups join the shared eBird pacing gate, and so do the List Comparer tab's two checklist lookups, the other caller on the same route that is outside it. (2) A successful checklist answer that has no observation list counts as a failed answer, on both transports, so it can no longer mark every species on that checklist "not in eBird's answer" for 30 days. (3) A saved answer or mark dated more than a day ahead of the clock reads as absent, in the escapee document and (by the v1.0.64 record's "one change for all three stores") in the hotspot-region and county-completeness stores. (4) The privacy policy's escapee retention sentence, and its neighbour in the same paragraph, are corrected as a HELD patch. The audit of every eBird lookup and the iCloud question are answered below; neither adds code to this run beyond item (1)'s List Comparer row.

## Why now
The 1.0.64 audit left these open as ideas `810282fa`, `fad789d8`, `7dd67e86` and `a3b2a269`; the user accepted all four and asked two questions: what else would cut eBird calls, and whether the cached answers should ride iCloud Sync. The pacing gap is the one with history: the escapee pass sends from a pool of four with no spacing and no shared cooldown, which helped turn the 1.0.62 test probes into 231 rate-limit refusals on one key. Two premises in the ideas were checked against the code and one is wrong (decisions.md D-02): web/Pi CAN produce the "answer with no observation list" shape, so that fix is twinned on both transports, not Tauri-only.

## User-facing impact
Default: none. Three small shifts. A cold escapee check on a large export takes about a second longer (the 150 ms spacing floor: 73 checklists, measured 9.7 s at four-wide in v0.5.87, now at least 11.0 s), and a rate-limited checklist is retried after a short wait instead of counting as failed and being re-asked next launch. The List Comparer tab shows its existing failure state, not an empty checklist, for a body eBird has never been seen to send. A clock that ran more than a day ahead costs one re-ask per affected answer once it is corrected. The privacy wording changes only after the user's yes.

## Design pass
Not needed: no visual change. No new in-app sentence is expected; if the Engineer finds one is needed it is in-app copy in the existing voice, written without a review stop.

## eBird call audit
Measured by reading every eBird URL site (12 in `lib/tauri/`, 13 in `backend/`) and every frontend caller of an eBird-backed path. Per-device figures use the reference export in `data/` (21,856 rows, 3,300 checklists, 8 states, 42 counties) and the v0.5.87 and v1.0.64 measurements.

## iCloud sync of cached answers
**Answer: not in this release, and probably only one store ever earns it.** Syncing a store is new persistence in the user's iCloud container: its own document (the native push reads a whole local file, and four of these stores live inside `settings.json`), a new item kind with its Rust twin, a merge validator, a Remove path, a Copy iCloud details line, privacy sentences, and verification only on the user's own signed-in devices (this Mac is not signed into iCloud; the v1.0.40 iPad pass took four builds). That crosses into New Feature territory (new data persisted, merge semantics needing schema discipline), so it is deferred to its own run whatever is chosen. It helps only the Mac, iPhone and iPad; Windows, Android and web/Pi gain nothing. Figures are per extra Apple device.

| Store | eBird calls saved per extra device | Document shape it would need | Clear-path obligations | Privacy sentence owed | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Projects answers `checklist-projects-v1` | About 3,300 once per year if that device runs the sweep (about 275 a month amortized); 0 if it never does | Own file; about 0.3 MB on the reference export (measured 90 B an entry), 13.2 MB worst case; merge is a clean union of raw fields, newest answer wins | Already registered; owes deleting every device's copy in iCloud on Clear and skipping the pull without a backup (day-obs precedent) | Holds the user's own checklist IDs and project memberships, so "they hold nothing from your own files" cannot be said; three iCloud sentences and the Clear sentence change; and the Projects section on a second device would show answers it never asked for | **The only one that pays.** Its own run if the user wants it |
| Escapee answers `exotic-provenance-v1` | About 110 once (73 cover plus the stuck species' 37), then about 37 a month (row D) | Own file; 21 KB on the reference export (79 checklists, 267 species), about 16.5 MB worst case; the hardest merge: ledger by newest, tokens by union under the per-species cap, stamps meaningful only under the cover that wrote them, and the two published lists must never merge (each device derives its own) | As above | As above, plus per-species provenance from the user's own checklists | **No**: small saving, riskiest merge; row D's root fix saves more |
| Hotspot regions `hotspot-regions-v1` | 8 a month | Up to 7.5 MB | As above | Public eBird data; two sentences | **No**: v1.0.62's "not synced" stands |
| County species lists `county-completeness-v1` | At most 42 a month | Settings-housed | As above | Public eBird data | **No** |
| Hotspot activity `hotspot-activity-v1` | Near zero: a 6-hour answer is usually stale on arrival | n/a | Not on the Clear path | n/a | **No** |
| County day lists `county-day-obs.json` | Already synced (v1.0.40) | n/a | n/a | n/a | n/a |

Is it asking for trouble? For everything but Projects, yes in proportion: the saving is tens of requests a month, and joining the gate (this run) already removes the burst risk that made those calls matter.

## What done looks like
- Fake-timer rows, red first, observing request starts on the client: every escapee and List Comparer checklist request starts at least the spacing floor after the previous gated start; a 429 opens the shared cooldown and is retried at most twice; no call waiting in the gate is sent after Stop or after Clear. The convergence row stays `[29, 8, 0, 0]` under both storage seams.
- A shared-fixture row on both transports: a successful body with no observation list writes no ledger entry and no stamp, and an empty list still stamps. In each of the three stores, an answer or mark exactly 24 h ahead is honoured and one past it reads as absent.
- The full vitest suite, `npm run build` and backend pytest pass; the held patch applies cleanly and turns its guards green, and is applied only after the user's yes, before 1.0.65 ships.
