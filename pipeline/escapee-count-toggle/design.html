What this direction decides

The control. "Count escapees" sits directly under "Count spuh, slash & hybrids" as an identical checkbox, so the two read as one count rule rather than a feature bolted beside a feature. The label names the class it governs and claims nothing about eBird; the parity claim is made once, in the rule line under the number, where it can be stated precisely.

The partial state. Automatic on tab open, with the plan stated as a definite figure before the first request goes out, a determinate bar, and a Stop control. The figure only ever falls, never rises, and it is muted while a pass is running so a settling number does not read as a final one.

The disclosure. Immediately under the figure, in the same region as the status. Every excluded species is named, links to its Species Detail record, and carries its evidence rather than a restatement of the rule. The list stays reachable with the toggle on.

Deliberate deviations from the Weft design doctrine

  • Type face. The doctrine asks for a distinctive display face and names Inter as the thing to avoid. SnowRaven's shipped face is Inter / system-ui across 61 versions, and design-system.md wins on specifics. Changing it here would be a reinvention of an app-wide surface inside a feature that adds one checkbox.
  • Palette. One dominant accent (--sr-accent) and heavily restrained colour is the brand's stated position, not an oversight. This feature mints no token and paints nothing new.
  • Depth. Flat surfaces with hairline borders are the shipped register. The new region deliberately has no card of its own, because a card inside a card is what the doctrine forbids and what this surface would otherwise become.
