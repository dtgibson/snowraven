## Feature Overview

A new configurable tab (working label **Family Tree**) that arranges the birder's own life list by eBird's classification, in two views over one model and one set of filters: a **rollup** of orders and families showing species seen of world species and photo, audio and video coverage, each family opening to its genera and species, and a **diagram** of the same order, family, genus and species hierarchy that zooms and pans and holds only the birder's own birds. Order, family, genus and family size come from a new bundled taxonomy asset; everything else is derived on the device from the two stored files and the escapee answer Statistics already caches.

The work lands in two slices, kept visible in the requirement grouping below. **Slice A** (the asset, the tab, the rollup, the filters and the links) is complete and shippable on its own. **Slice B** (the diagram and find) is built on Slice A's working model. The Ship set at the end applies to the feature as a whole.

## User Stories

> **US-01**: As a birder with a life list in the hundreds or thousands, I want to see how my species spread across the bird families, each with how many of the world's species in that family I have seen, so that I can see the shape of my list instead of a flat count.

> **US-02**: As a birder who uploads to the Macaulay Library, I want each family to show how many of my species in it I have photographed, recorded and filmed, so that I can see which families my media is thin in.

> **US-03**: As a birder who thinks in places and years, I want to narrow the whole view to one country, state or province, or county, to one year, or to the species that have or lack a photo, audio recording or video, so that I can ask the same question of a county list, a year list or my media gaps.

> **US-04**: As a birder who likes the Birds of the World family tree, I want a tree of only my own species that I can zoom from the whole tree down to readable species names, so that I can explore my own birds the way that tree lets me explore all of them.

> **US-05**: As a birder following a thread, I want every species name to open that species in SnowRaven and carry its eBird and Birds of the World links, and every family to offer its Birds of the World page, so that I can go from my tree to the account in one press.

> **US-06**: As a keyboard or screen-reader user, I want everything the diagram offers (every level, count and link) available in a view I can read and operate without a pointer, so that the tab works for me at 320 px and 200% text.

> **US-07**: As a birder who checks the numbers, I want this tab's species to agree with Life Lists and Statistics at the same settings, and any name SnowRaven cannot place to be shown as such, so that I can trust the totals and nothing is silently dropped or guessed.

> **US-08**: As a user who reorders and hides tabs, I want the new tab to join the tab roster like any other, so that I can place it where I want or hide it, and my saved layout is kept.

## Functional Requirements

Terms used below. The **roster** is the set of species the tab counts under the current controls, each keyed by its normalized common name exactly as Life Lists keys it. The **scope** is the current Region and Year. A **placed** species is one the bundled taxonomy assigns to a family; an **unplaced** one is on the roster and has no family here. The **model** is the one derived structure (orders, families, genera, species, counts, unplaced group) both views read. **Life Lists at the same settings** means Life Lists' own derivation with the same Region, the same Include Escapees state, Include Exotics on, and the media buttons named. `{Label}` in a default string stands for the tab's final label (Open Question 4).

Default strings are given so two engineers produce the same text. The Designer may reword any of them at Stage 4; the meaning and the conditions are fixed here.

## Slice A: asset, tab, rollup, filters, links

### A1. The family taxonomy asset

> **FR-01**: The build shall add one build-time asset (recommended `frontend/src/assets/ebird-families.json`) derived by the existing annual taxonomy generator (`scripts/build-ebird-taxonomy.mjs`, pure core under `scripts/lib/`) from the same single eBird taxonomy response that run already fetches. It shall add no second request, no new build-time host and no step to `npm run build`.

> **FR-02**: The asset shall carry, and the tab shall read from it and from the committed snapshot only: for every family in the taxonomy its code, common name, scientific name, order, position in taxonomic sequence and world species count (FR-04); for every order its name and position; for every species-category taxon its family, its genus and its current scientific name; the species eBird marks extinct, if the response marks them (Open Question 3); and the snapshot version it was built against. Families and orders the birder has never recorded are present in the asset as denominators only.

> **FR-03**: The generator shall fail closed: it shall write nothing (this asset or any other in the run) unless every species-category taxon has exactly one family and one order, every family belongs to exactly one order, every family code passes the family-code shape the runtime link guard uses (FR-50), every family and order name is a non-empty string within a length bound the schema states, the per-family species tallies sum to the number of species-category taxa, and the asset's species set equals the species set of the snapshot the same run writes. The run's existing revision check and its nothing-is-written-until-every-check-passes posture are unchanged.

> **FR-04**: A family's **world species count** shall be the number of species-category taxa in that family in the bundled taxonomy that eBird does not mark extinct, plus the number of extinct species in that family that appear on the birder's roster at Everywhere, all years, with Include Escapees on. It is therefore fixed for a loaded backup, never changes with Region, Year, Media or Include Escapees, and is never smaller than the family's species seen under any control state. An order's world species count is the sum over its families. The world family total and the world order total count only the families and orders whose world species count is above zero, which is the species rule one level up: a wholly extinct family or order counts on both sides exactly when the birder has a species of it, and otherwise on neither, so no family sits in the denominator where no birder can reach it (D-05; Open Question 18 holds the reversal).

> **FR-05**: The asset shall be loaded lazily, by dynamic import, the first time the tab mounts, and shall stay off the entry chunk. As a Vite-inlined build-time asset it is defended at build time (FR-03) and in CI (FR-06), not by runtime validation; if it ever becomes runtime-loaded the durable-cache rule applies in full. It ships in the frontend only: no backend twin, no backend route.

> **FR-06**: A CI suite that makes no request shall hold the committed asset to the committed snapshot by an independent re-derivation: the asset's species names equal the key set of the snapshot's `byCom` map, as a set, in both directions and with no duplicate (the asset is keyed by lowercase common name and is the only taxonomy file the tab loads, so this is the species-category equivalence stated over names, and it supersedes the mention of the snapshot's name maps as a runtime lookup source in FR-02 and FR-24; D-04); each family's species are contiguous in the snapshot's taxonomic order and each order's families are contiguous; each stored world count equals the count recomputed from the species-to-family map and the extinct set; the asset's recorded version equals the snapshot's; and every family code passes the runtime shape guard, the two declarations compared to each other and never restated. A generator suite over a fixture response shall show each FR-03 condition stopping the run with nothing written.

> **FR-07**: In the same change, `scripts/taxonomy-history-input/README.md` shall gain the family asset in its ordered annual refresh (regenerated in the snapshot run; its re-derive step covers every figure measured on the old asset), and `.claude/rules/bird-names.md` shall gain `paths` entries for the asset, the generator core, the tab's `lib/` modules and its component folder. Any module that holds a declared scan (NFR-10) is added to the `security.md` glob in the same change.

### A2. Tab registration and placement

> **FR-08**: The app shall register a new configurable tab (recommended key `family-tree`, label **Family Tree**, D-24) in `TAB_LABELS` and `DEFAULT_TAB_ORDER`, placed between `breeding-codes` and `hotspot-reports` in the default order (the user's direction, D-13), so the default order reads Weather, Statistics, Map Explorer, Species Detail, Calendar, Targets, Multimedia, Breeding Codes, Family Tree, Hotspot Reports, Checklists, Life Lists, List Comparer, Named Birds, with an entry in `lib/tabIcons.tsx`. The tab's component and model modules shall carry names distinct from the Life Lists and Multimedia files.

> **FR-09**: The tab shall be hideable and reorderable through the existing Settings tab-layout UI with no change to that UI, and shall appear in the sidebar, the icon rail, the phone bottom bar's More sheet and Search's tab results through the existing registry. Its component shall be lazy, loading with the tab as the other tabs do.

> **FR-10**: A saved tab order that equals the shipped default from before this feature (element for element, after normalization and the missing-tab append) shall read as the new default, which places Family Tree between Breeding Codes and Hotspot Reports (FR-08); any other saved order is a custom order and shall be kept as saved, with every tab it lacks appended after its last tab in default order, so an order that holds every pre-feature tab gets Family Tree last; the saved hidden set shall be preserved; nothing shall be written back. Exactly one generation migrates: `PREVIOUS_DEFAULT_TAB_ORDER` now holds the pre-feature default and the order it used to hold is retired and reads as a custom order. One side effect of that retirement is accepted and named here: a device that saved the default from before Hotspot Reports and has not saved a layout since has been reading as the current default, and from this release reads as a custom order, so on that device the saved tabs keep their order and the two tabs that order lacks are appended in default order, Family Tree and then Hotspot Reports: Hotspot Reports moves from its default place to the very end, with Family Tree directly before it. Nothing is lost or hidden, and Restore defaults puts it back.

> **FR-11**: Because the tab adds a destination to the phone More sheet, the sheet shall be re-measured in WebKit at 393 by 852 with `.sr-ios-app` and a 34 px bottom inset, and both terms of its cap and the guard's pixel figure in `TabNav.test.tsx` shall move together so every destination still shows at the default text size with nothing to scroll and Settings stays pinned.

> **FR-12**: The feature shall leave unchanged: Life Lists, Multimedia and Statistics as the user sees them; the Statistics escapee check's requests, cache, status line and the rule that only Statistics may start it. The tab's modules shall join the passive side of `exoticProvenanceGraph.test.ts`. Sharing a Life Lists derivation function with this tab is permitted only where Life Lists' output is byte-identical before and after.

### A3. Loading and states

> **FR-13**: With no eBird backup saved, the tab shall show the app's standard setup panel: title "eBird Backup Required", body "The {Label} tab loads automatically from your stored eBird backup. You haven't saved one yet.", the shared eBird backup steps and the shared go-to-Settings action. No controls, rollup or diagram render.

> **FR-14**: With a backup saved that cannot be read or parsed, the tab shall show the shared tab load-error alert with the shared eBird backup message, as Life Lists does, and not the setup panel. If the family asset fails to load, the tab shall show the same alert with the constant message "SnowRaven couldn't load its bird family data. Reload to try again." and one action, Reload, in place of the alert's go-to-Settings action, and no rollup or diagram; it shall never present every species as unplaced. Reload restarts the app's page, as the app's error screen does. The failure stands for the rest of the session: showing the tab again starts no load, because a failed chunk is not requested again by the browser. The alert region shall be mounted, empty, in every branch that can reach an error, and its messages shall be a closed set of compile-time constants.

> **FR-15**: While the backup or the asset is loading the tab shall show the shared loading indicator with an accessible status label. A load or derivation that is superseded (a file replaced, the tab unmounted, a newer derivation started) shall write no state.

> **FR-16**: The ML export shall be optional and loaded through the shared ML export cache. An absent or unreadable export shall not block or change the species figures; its effect on media figures and the Media filter is FR-35.

> **FR-17**: The tab shall re-derive everything it shows whenever the data-file epoch changes (either file saved, replaced or removed by any writer, a delete or an iCloud clear included), with the files epoch in its load effect's dependencies. Clearing all data shall leave the setup state. After a re-derive, a selected Year the new backup does not contain shall fall back to all years, and a selected Region it does not contain shall fall back to Everywhere, as Life Lists handles a missing region.

### A4. The species set and its figures

> **FR-18**: The roster at all years shall be Life Lists' own derivation, not a second one: the same countable fold (`isNonCountableForm` as the only countability predicate, `normalizeSpeciesName` folding a subspecies-level form into its parent), the same valid-date rule, and the same region index and regional rule. The tab shall obtain it by calling the functions Life Lists calls, or one function both tabs call.

> **FR-19**: With a Year selected, the roster shall be exactly the species that have at least one countable, validly dated observation in that calendar year inside the current Region. The union of the per-year rosters over every year present in the Region shall equal the all-years roster for that Region.

> **FR-20**: The tab shall read escapee classification passively, through the same reader Life Lists uses (`lifeListsClassification` over the cached provenance snapshot, with the one taxonomy join from `loadEscapeeJoin()`), and apply it exactly as Life Lists does: with **Include Escapees** off the roster excludes exactly the names that reader returns as escapees; with it on they are included. That reader's rule is stated over the observations Statistics consulted, and this PRD adds no requirement over every carrier or every checklist. Naturalized and provisional exotics are always counted, as at Life Lists' default. A species whose status is unresolved is counted. The tab shall never start, retry or extend the check. The reader is used exactly as it stands (`useLifeListsProvenance`): its own confirmation of the published names, which it re-runs when the provenance snapshot changes, is existing Life Lists behavior that this feature neither changes nor copies (D-08; see NFR-03).

> **FR-21**: **Reconciliation.** For every Region choice, each Include Escapees state and each Media selection made only of "has" constraints, with Year at all years, the tab's roster shall equal, as a set of names, the rows Life Lists produces at the same settings (same region, same Include Escapees, Include Exotics on, the media buttons matching the "has" kinds). At Everywhere, all years, no Media constraint and Include Escapees off this is Life Lists at its defaults, and so the Statistics species figure at its defaults.

> **FR-22**: Media facts shall come from the ML export through Life Lists' asset derivation. An **item** is an ML row with a valid date whose normalized common name is a roster species; a species **has** a kind in scope when it has at least one item of that kind in scope. Scope applies to media as it does to sightings (Open Question 8): under a Region an item counts by Life Lists' regional rule (its checklist is in the backup and lies inside the region; an item with no checklist, or one the backup does not hold, counts only under Everywhere); under a Year an item counts when its own date falls in that year. At Everywhere and all years, has-a-kind equals Life Lists' media presence for that species.

> **FR-23**: When Statistics' escapee check is not complete for the loaded export, by Statistics' own completion signal as Life Lists reads it (`statisticsCheckComplete`, the `remainingSpecies` test, never whether every checklist is in the consulted ledger), the tab shall show one standing sentence below the controls: "Escapee status comes from the escapee check on Statistics, which has not finished for this export, so species it has not settled are counted." It is plain text, has no link or button and triggers nothing. When the check is complete no sentence shows.

### A5. Placement on the classification

> **FR-24**: Each roster species shall be placed by looking its name up in the bundled taxonomy (the committed snapshot's name maps and the FR-01 asset), never from text in the backup beyond that lookup key, and never from a live or refreshed taxonomy, so the tree is the same online and offline and on every platform for the same build and files. Every lookup of a user-file-derived string in a bundled object shall go through `Object.hasOwn` or a `Map`.

> **FR-25**: A roster species the lookup cannot place shall appear in a **Not placed** group, in both views' totals and listed by name in the rollup, with the reason "eBird's taxonomy in this version of SnowRaven does not list this name, so it has no family here." It shall never be dropped and never assigned a family by guess, by scientific-name text or by the taxonomy history (Open Question 14). Unplaced species count toward the roster and the headline species figure and toward no family, order or genus. The group is omitted when it is empty.

> **FR-26**: The tab shall show one line stating how many distinct recorded names in scope are not single species (the names `isNonCountableForm` rejects: spuhs, slashes, hybrids and the other forms eBird does not count): "{N} other {entry | entries} in your backup {is | are} not a single species, so {it has | they have} no place on the tree." The line is omitted at zero and obeys count-of-one agreement through the copy module (NFR-13).

> **FR-27**: A placed species' genus, family, order and displayed scientific name shall come from the bundled taxonomy, so an older export that names a since-moved genus still draws one consistent tree. An unplaced species' scientific name, where shown, comes from the backup through Life Lists' existing bounded binomial read.

### A6. Filters and controls

> **FR-28**: **Region** shall be the Life Lists region picker (Everywhere, then the countries, states or provinces, and counties present in the backup, in that grouping), reused as a component or through its choice builder, with an explicit accessible name.

> **FR-29**: **Year** shall offer all years (the default) and each calendar year that has at least one validly dated countable observation in the backup, newest first, in a control with an explicit accessible name. The year is read from the parsed date through Life Lists' existing bounded day read; no `Date` is constructed in a render body.

> **FR-30**: **Media** shall let the user constrain each of Photo, Audio and Video independently to one of three states: no constraint (the default), has, or lacks. A species passes when it meets every constraint that is set: "has" as FR-22 defines it, "lacks" as its negation within the roster for the current scope and Include Escapees state. The control's shape is the Designer's (Open Question 7); every state shall be exposed to assistive technology and no state shall be carried by color alone.

> **FR-31**: **Include Escapees** shall render through the app's `ToggleSwitch`, off by default, with its state announced to assistive technology.

> **FR-32**: The chosen Region, the chosen view (FR-53) and the chosen Names state (FR-80) shall be remembered through the `storage` seam, each under its own setting key, validated on read (an unparseable or no-longer-present value reads as the default and nothing is written back). Year, Media, Include Escapees, the rollup's sort and every open or expanded state are session-only: they persist while the app runs (tabs stay mounted) and reset on relaunch. Nothing derived is stored, and nothing joins `clearDerived.ts`. The rotate note's dismissal (FR-85) is the one other thing the tab writes.

> **FR-33**: Every filter shall apply to both views through the one model: a control change produces one new model and both views render from it; neither view computes a count the model does not hold.

> **FR-34**: When the roster is empty the tab shall show one sentence in place of the views: with every filter at its default, "No countable species in your eBird backup yet."; otherwise, "No species match these filters." The controls stay available.

> **FR-35**: With a backup loaded and no ML export saved, the media figures shall be omitted (not shown as zero) and the Media filter shall render disabled, with its state exposed to assistive technology, beside the sentence "Photo, audio, and video figures need your Macaulay Library export, saved in Settings under Default Files." With an export saved that could not be read, the same omission and disabled state shall apply beside "SnowRaven couldn't read your Macaulay Library export, so photo, audio, and video figures are unavailable; re-upload it in Settings under Default Files." In both states the species figures and the other controls work normally.

### A7. The rollup view

> **FR-36**: The rollup shall open with a headline stating, for the current roster: species seen against the world species total, families seen against the world family total, and orders seen against the world order total. The species figure is the full roster count, unplaced species included; the family and order figures count families and orders with at least one roster species.

> **FR-37**: Below the headline the rollup shall list every order and family that has at least one roster species, and no others. Each family row shall show: the family's name as the Names control sets it (FR-78, FR-79; both names in the Both state); species seen and the family's world species count; and, when an ML export is loaded and readable, the number of its seen species that have a photo, that have audio and that have video, and the number of items of each kind, all in scope (FR-22). Each order shall show its name under the same control, species seen of world species, and families seen of the order's world families. How "seen of world" is drawn (number, bar or both) is the Designer's; the two numbers are always present as text.

> **FR-38**: World counts shall be the same under every control state (FR-04). Whenever Region is not Everywhere, Year is not all years or a Media constraint is set, the tab shall show: "Family totals are world species and do not change with the filters."

> **FR-39**: **Sum invariants**, in every control state and in both views: the families' species seen plus the Not placed count equals the roster count and the headline species figure; each order's species seen equals the sum over its families; each family's species seen equals the sum over its genera; for each kind, a family's species-with-that-kind is at most its species seen and is greater than zero exactly when its item count for that kind is greater than zero; and with a single "has" constraint set, each family's species seen equals its species-with-that-kind.

> **FR-40**: The default order shall be taxonomic: orders in taxonomic sequence, families in sequence within their order. The user shall be able to sort families by family name, species seen, share of world species seen and, when media figures show, species with each kind. Under any sort other than taxonomic the families are listed flat, each row naming its order, with ties broken by taxonomic sequence so the order is identical across reloads and platforms. The sort control exposes its current state to assistive technology.

> **FR-41**: Each family row shall be a disclosure that opens to that family's genera, in taxonomic sequence, each genus showing its name and species seen, and under each genus its roster species in taxonomic sequence. The Not placed group (FR-25) opens the same way to its species in name order. Opening or closing a row shall not move keyboard focus to the document body.

> **FR-42**: Each species row shall render the name through `<BirdName>` in every Names state (FR-81 says what each state means for it), with `hasEntry` true, the scientific name from FR-27, `taxonCode` for the eBird and Birds of the World marks, and `onOpenSpecies` so the name opens the species on Species Detail. When an ML export is loaded and readable, the row shall carry Life Lists' passive media presence cell (three fixed slots in Photo, Audio, Video order, with an accessible label naming the kinds present or "No media"), read in scope (FR-22).

> **FR-43**: The contents of a closed family shall be absent from the tab order and from the accessibility tree (conditionally rendered, or `inert` with the literal attribute asserted in both states). Each disclosure control shall expose `aria-expanded` and name its family.

> **FR-44**: Counts shall be formatted with locale grouping. A displayed share shall go through `fmtSharePct`, so a nonzero share never reads as a bare "0%". Every count-bearing string shall be built in the tab's copy module.

> **FR-45**: Every DOM id and ARIA IDREF the tab mints, in either view, shall be keyed on an index, never on a species name, a family name or code, a region or any other value read from a file or the asset.

### A8. Links

> **FR-46**: Species links are exactly `<BirdName>`'s: the in-app Species Detail link and the eBird and Birds of the World marks, drawn only when the species' taxon code is resolved and passes `SPECIES_CODE_RE`. A pending or failed code resolve renders names without marks and never blocks, delays or reorders either view.

> **FR-47**: Each family shall offer a link to its Birds of the World family account, built from the family code, only if that URL shape has been verified (Open Question 1). An unverified shape ships as no link: the family name renders as plain text and no control that looks pressable is drawn in its place.

> **FR-48**: Each family may offer a link to the birder's own Macaulay Library media for that family, built on `ML_CATALOG_BASE` through the existing catalog-link builder with the family code as `taxonCode`, only if the catalog is verified to accept a family code (Open Question 2) and only where the existing own-media links would render. An unverified shape ships as no link.

> **FR-49**: Orders and genera shall have no outside link. Their actions are in-app only (FR-41 in the rollup; FR-58 in the diagram), per Open Question 11.

> **FR-50**: Every new outbound link shall: gate its code on a named shape guard (the family code against the family-code shape, one definition shared with FR-03 and FR-06); wrap the code in `encodeURIComponent`; render through `OutboundLink`, or call `openNewTabLink(event, href)` with the same expression it renders as `href`; carry an accessible name of the form "Open {family common name} on Birds of the World (opens in a new tab)"; target only a host the app already links to; and have a runtime row that clicks it through `frontend/src/test/tauriOpener.ts`. A code that fails its guard renders no link and the row stays.

### A9. Network

> **FR-51**: The tab shall make no third-party request, add no endpoint or host, and move no request between components. The requests it may cause are exactly: the two stored-file reads through the `storage` seam and its own lazy chunks, the family asset among them (on web and Pi these go to the user's own server); and at most one batched `/taxonomy/codes` resolve per distinct roster name set through `transport.post`, in the shape Life Lists uses, for the link marks and the FR-23 completion check (answered on web and Pi by the user's own server and in the native apps by the local taxonomy service, which may start the existing, already disclosed taxonomy refresh). No control change, view switch, zoom, pan, expand or find causes a request. Requests a system component makes from the app's process count: the tab mounts no frame and loads no font, script or image beyond the favicons `<BirdName>`'s marks already load on every tab with bird names.

## Slice B: the diagram and find

### B1. The two views

> **FR-52**: The tab shall offer a view control with two options, the rollup and the diagram, rendered as a segmented control with `aria-pressed`. Its stored values shall be label-agnostic (recommended `'rollup'` and `'diagram'`).

> **FR-53**: At first use, meaning while no view choice is stored, the tab shall open on the diagram (the tree) when the window is wider than the still tier (section C4) and on the rollup (the table) at or under it, where the tree is a still behind a button and the table is the working view (D-35, correcting D-25). That default is read when the tab opens; it does not switch the view under the user if the window later crosses the tier, and it writes nothing. After the user's first change of view the choice is stored (FR-32) and the tab opens on it on every tier. Where the diagram is withheld on a platform (FR-71), the tab opens on the rollup there.

> **FR-54**: Switching views shall keep every filter and shall not re-derive the model. The diagram's library and renderer shall load the first time the diagram is shown, not with the tab (NFR-06).

### B2. Diagram content

> **FR-55**: The diagram shall draw the model's hierarchy and nothing else: a node for each order, family, genus and species with at least one roster species beneath or at it, joined as a tree. It shall draw no unseen order, family, genus or species, and no ghost or placeholder for one. Its form (branch direction, radial or other) is the Designer's (Open Question 9). This requirement holds over the diagram's node data, not over what is mounted at one moment (D-10): what is mounted is a window onto that data, bounded by NFR-04. A label that is drawn is always at the app's body text size at the current in-app text scale; zoom changes how much room each node has, never the size of the text. A node with room for one label line is drawn with its label; a run of sibling nodes with less room than that is drawn as one count of the nodes in the run, in text. Every species in the node data stays reachable by expanding, zooming or find.

> **FR-56**: Every node's label shall be real text naming its taxon. Each order and family node shall carry its rollup figures from the model (species seen and world species count; the media figures when they show), visible on the node or revealed for the active node without relying on hover alone.

> **FR-57**: Each species leaf shall show the species' name as the Names control sets it (FR-78, FR-79) and, when an ML export is loaded and readable, its media state for the three kinds in scope, with position or shape carrying identity and not color alone. The name shall render through `<BirdName>` with the FR-42 props, on the leaf or in a readout for the active leaf (Open Question 13), so opening the species on Species Detail and both link marks are reachable from every leaf.

> **FR-58**: Every order, family and genus node shall expand and collapse, by pointer and by keyboard. The diagram shall offer a control that fits the whole drawn tree in view and a control that returns to the arrival state. The arrival expansion is the Designer's, within NFR-04. Expansion state is session-only, is kept for a taxon that leaves the model under a filter and returns, and is independent of the rollup's open rows.

> **FR-59**: Beside the diagram, in text, the tab shall state the Not placed count (when not zero) with a way to reach the rollup's Not placed group, and the FR-26 line, so the diagram's node counts plus the stated Not placed count equal the headline species figure. The FR-34 empty sentence replaces the diagram when the roster is empty.

### B3. Zoom and pan

> **FR-60**: The diagram shall zoom between a whole-tree fit and a level at which species names are at least the app's body text size at the current in-app text scale. Zoom in, Zoom out and Fit shall be buttons rendered through the `Button` primitive with accessible names, operable by pointer, touch and keyboard. Zoom shall never be gesture-only.

> **FR-61**: With focus in the diagram, `+` (and `=`) shall zoom in, `-` shall zoom out and `0` shall fit. These keys act only while focus is inside the diagram.

> **FR-62**: A two-finger pinch on a touch screen and a trackpad pinch shall zoom the diagram about the gesture's center. Wheel behavior follows Open Question 10. A pinch that begins inside the diagram shall zoom the diagram and shall not leave the page itself zoomed; the feature shall add no `maximum-scale` or `user-scalable` clamp to the viewport, so page zoom stays available everywhere else.

> **FR-63**: Dragging shall pan the diagram. A mouse drag follows the pointer one to one, with no threshold, easing, snapping or motion after release. The active node is always brought into view, so a keyboard user pans by moving through nodes.

> **FR-64**: Zoom and pan are view state only: they change no count, no filter and nothing stored, and cause no request. A filter change keeps the view where the active node still exists and otherwise fits. Any animated zoom, fit or pan shall be instant when `prefers-reduced-motion` matches at the moment of the action.

### B4. Find

> **FR-65**: The tab shall offer a find control, in both views, that matches what the user types against the names in the current model, at all four levels (orders are included now that they carry a name, D-24): species common and scientific names, genus scientific names and derived common names (FR-83), family common and scientific names, and order scientific names and authored common names (FR-82). Find follows the Names control (FR-78): each result leads with the name that control shows for it, with the other name beneath where the control shows both, a match on the leading name ranks ahead of a match on the other name, and each result states its level (Species, Genus, Family or Order). The input's accessible name and its placeholder shall both read "Find a species, genus, family, or order", and the list's accessible name shall name the same four levels. It shall be the house combobox shape (one `role="combobox"` input with a `role="listbox"` of `role="option"` rows selected through `aria-activedescendant`, option ids keyed on the index), with an explicit accessible name, and should reuse the shared species matching so four-letter codes match as they do in the species pickers.

> **FR-66**: Choosing a result shall reveal that taxon in the current view. In the diagram: expand its ancestors, bring it into view at a readable zoom and make it the active node. In the rollup: return to taxonomic order if needed, open its family, scroll it into view and move focus to it; for an order, scroll its heading into view and move focus to its first family row. In the phone tier's in-page tree, which is a still (FR-87), choosing a result opens the tree full screen and reveals the taxon there. Find changes no filter and no count.

> **FR-67**: When nothing matches, the list shall show "Nothing matches this search." The sentence names no level, since Find returns four of them (D-25). Escape with the list open closes the list only, consumed at the element while the list is open; with it closed Escape keeps bubbling.

### B5. Keyboard and assistive technology in the diagram

> **FR-68**: The diagram itself shall be one tab stop, with the active node exposed through `aria-activedescendant` and index-keyed ids (Open Question 12). With focus on the diagram, arrow keys move the active node along the tree, and Enter or Space expands or collapses a branch or opens a species. No node holds focusable content: a species leaf's label is plain text, and while a leaf is the active node a readout beside the diagram renders that species through `<BirdName>` with the FR-42 props (D-11, closing Open Question 13). The readout follows the diagram in the tab order, and the tab stops it adds are a count fixed by the kind of active node (none, species, genus, family or order) and by whether that node's code is resolved and its outside links verified, never by the roster; the counts are the table in `design-spec.md` section 9.9, which governs and supersedes D-11's "at most three". The tab stops the diagram view adds are therefore the toolbar controls, the one diagram stop and the readout's count for the active node, a total that does not grow with the roster. No node action is pointer-only. The rollup renders every species through `<BirdName>` (FR-42), and the plain-text leaf is recorded as an exception in `.claude/rules/bird-names.md` in the same change.

> **FR-69**: Each node's accessible name shall state its level, its name and its figures (for a family, "Family, {common name}, {seen} of {world} species"; for a leaf, its name and the kinds of media it has). The diagram's accessible description shall say that the rollup view holds the same information as a list.

> **FR-70**: The active node shall carry a visible focus indication that does not rely on color alone and is never hidden under the diagram's own controls or any sticky band.

### B6. Platform gating

> **FR-71**: The diagram shall ship on Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad, Android and the self-hosted web version unless a measurement on a platform's engine shows it cannot meet NFR-02, NFR-04 or FR-60 to FR-64 there. In that case the diagram is withheld on that platform behind one named predicate in `lib/platformGates.ts` (never a raw `isIOS()` or `isAndroid()`), the view control is not drawn there, the rollup and find carry the feature, and the decision is written in `decisions.md` with the measurement, what would reverse it and where the gate lives. Help states which platforms draw the diagram only if any is gated.

## Design review additions (D-13 to D-24)

Each requirement names the slice it lands with. They sit here, after both slices, only so that no earlier ID moves.

### C1. The Names control

> **FR-78** (Slice A): The tab shall offer one **Names** control with three states, **Common**, **Scientific** and **Both**, rendered as a segmented control with `aria-pressed` in the app's existing compact style (D-22), with label-agnostic stored values (recommended `'common'`, `'scientific'`, `'both'`). It is one control for the whole tab: it sets how names read at all four levels (order, family, genus, species) in the rollup, in the diagram and in Find's results. Changing it changes no count, no filter, no order of rows and no expansion, and causes no request.

> **FR-79** (Slice A): The three states shall read as follows. **Both**: the common name leads and the scientific name sits beneath it (or after it where the form is a single line). **Common**: the common name alone, and where a taxon has no common name (a genus the FR-83 rule gives none) its scientific name shows in its place. **Scientific**: the scientific name alone, and where a taxon has no scientific name to show (an unplaced species whose backup row gives none) its common name shows in its place. No row or node is ever nameless. Every accessible name carries the same names the eye sees in the current state. The readout for the active node always shows both names with the common name leading, in every state (Open Question 21).

> **FR-80** (Slice A): The control shall open on **Both** (D-22, D-23) and the choice shall be remembered through the `storage` seam under a third validated setting key of this tab's (named by the pattern of the Region and view keys; the exact key is the Architect's), read as FR-32 reads the other two: a missing, unparseable or unknown value reads as Both and nothing is written back.

Known consequence, stated for the record and not as a requirement: on the approved mockup, Both shows about a third fewer birds per screen in the tree than a single name does. The user accepted that cost in choosing Both as the opening state (D-23).

> **FR-81** (Slice A): In the rollup every species still renders through `<BirdName>` in every Names state; the bird-names rule is unchanged. **Both** is FR-42's rendering as first written: the common name as the link, the scientific name beneath. **Common** is the same without the scientific line. **Scientific-first** means the text of the link that opens the species reads as the scientific name and no second line shows, while everything else about the name stays what it is in the other states: the species it opens on Species Detail (`onOpenSpecies` receives the same normalized common name), `hasEntry`, the taxon code, and the eBird and Birds of the World marks with the same hrefs and the same accessible names, which keep naming the bird by its common name. How `<BirdName>` is made to lead with a scientific name is the Architect's (a prop on the component, or another route through it); it shall not be a hand-rolled name beside hand-placed marks, and every other tab's `<BirdName>` output shall be byte-identical before and after.

### C2. Common names for orders and genera

> **FR-82** (Slice A): Every order in the asset shall carry a short plain-English common name from one authored table, with the wording the user approved at the design review (the mockup's order names, D-21 and D-23; "Waterfowl" for Anseriformes and "Perching Birds" for Passeriformes are two of them). The table is authored data shipped in the bundled asset or beside it (the Architect's choice), never fetched and never derived from the user's files. The generator and the CI equivalence suite shall hold the table to the asset as a set, in both directions: a run or a suite fails when an order in the asset has no authored name, when the table names an order the asset lacks, or when a name is empty, over the schema's length bound or holds a control character. The annual runbook (FR-07) shall say that a new order stops the generator until its name is authored.

> **FR-83** (Slice A): A genus shall carry a common name only where the following rule gives one, and none otherwise (D-21, written out in D-25). The rule runs in the generator, at build time, over the asset's world list, never over the user's files and never at run time. **Who qualifies:** a genus for which the asset holds two or more species-category taxa, extinct ones included. **The group word:** each species' whole last word of its common name, a hyphenated last word counting whole (Screech-Owl, Crag-Martin); only if those do not all agree, the part after the last hyphen of each last word. **The name:** when every species of the genus agrees on the group word, that word in the plural, title-cased. Plurals are formed by the regular endings (s, es, ies) and by one closed list of exceptions in the generator: unchanged for fowl, grouse and quail; goose to geese; titmouse to titmice; monarch to monarchs; bird-of-paradise and cock-of-the-rock pluralized on their first word. **No name:** when the species do not agree, when the genus has one species, or when the group word is the genus name itself (Vireo, Junco). A plural the list cannot form well is given an entry in the list or the genus is left without a name through a second closed list in the generator, decided at the FR-91 review; it is never guessed and never formed at run time. The rule is deterministic (the same taxonomy derives the same bytes), no name is authored for a single genus outside those two lists, two genera may carry the same name, and no id, key or lookup is ever built on one (FR-45). The CI suite shall re-derive the rule independently of the generator for every genus in the asset, in both directions: a genus that carries a name satisfies the rule and carries the plural the rule and the exception list give, and a genus that satisfies the rule carries a name unless it is on the left-unnamed list.

### C3. Expand all and Collapse all in the rollup

> **FR-84** (Slice A): The rollup shall offer **Expand all** and **Collapse all**, the same pair the diagram's toolbar has, as one pair of controls acting on the view that is showing (D-15). In the rollup, Expand all opens every family, and the Not placed group, to its genera and species; Collapse all closes them all; order headings are headings and stay. Both render through the `Button` primitive with accessible names, keep focus on the pressed control, and leave the diagram's expansion untouched (FR-58). FR-43 still holds for whatever is closed. Expand all shall not freeze the tab on a life list of several thousand species: the Architect's schema states its measured cost at a roster of every species in the asset and the bound it is held to.

### C4. The phone tier

**The tier, defined once.** "The phone tier" in FR-85 to FR-88, and "the still tier" in FR-53, are one thing: the shared full-screen hook's `stillTier`, the single query `(max-width: 640px)`, which is the app's established phone boundary. There is no second breakpoint, and this build reads the tier from the hook and does not restate the width (D-26, D-27). It is a window width, not a device: a desktop window at or under it is in the tier, and a phone in landscape wider than it is not.

> **FR-85** (Slice B): On the phone tier the tab shall show the app's existing rotate-or-use-a-larger-screen note (the `ChartViewTip` component, reused and not copied), above the tree, with the body "The tree gets more room in landscape. Rotate your device for a wider view, or open SnowRaven's desktop app if you have it." Its dismissal is this tab's own and is remembered the way the existing notes' are: its own flag in the same setting, written through the `storage` seam by a merge that leaves the other pages' flags as they are, and never shown before the saved flags have loaded. The note is absent above the phone tier.

> **FR-86** (Slice A): On the phone tier the rollup shall have no vertical scroller of its own: it flows with the page, and the page is the only thing a vertical swipe moves (D-17, D-20).

> **FR-87** (Slice B): On the phone tier the diagram shall show in the page as a still, following the maps' shared contract (D-27): a wrapper that is a named image (`role="img"` with an accessible name) around the tree drawn from its top at one line per order (the whole tree where that fits), in a box that does not scroll, with a fade at its bottom edge where the tree continues; the box is `inert` and holds no focusable content. This is the still the user approved and is not the toolbar's Fit, which stays a true fit of the whole drawn tree (FR-58). Only the box's content is absent from the accessibility tree; the wrapper is in it, as one image with its name. A swipe that lands on the still moves the page. Beneath it an **Open the tree** button (the `Button` primitive, at the touch-target size) and the sentence "It opens in fullscreen, where it scrolls and zooms on its own." Pressing the button, or the still itself, opens the tree full screen (FR-88). The button is the keyboard and screen-reader path; the still adds no function the button lacks. The diagram's own tools, its full-screen toggle and its readout show only in full screen on this tier; the view control, the Names control, the filters and Find stay in the page.

> **FR-88** (Slice B): The diagram shall have a full-screen toggle in its toolbar on every tier. In full screen the tree's box is the only scroller. An Exit control and Escape leave it (with Find's list open, Escape closes the list first, FR-67), and focus returns to the control that opened it. Zoom, place, expansion and the active node are kept across entering and leaving full screen, in both directions. In a window wider than the still tier, at any in-app text scale, 150% and 200% included, where the layout takes its narrow form, the tree stays live in the page and is not replaced by the still; at or under the tier, on any device, the tree in the page is the still (D-27, narrowing D-20); its box is never taller than the window, and a press or focus that lands on a box only partly in view brings the whole box into view before anything else happens.

> **FR-89** (Slice B): The tree's full screen shall behave as the maps' does, by using the app's existing embedded-map full-screen mechanism (`lib/useMapFullscreen.ts` and `.sr-map-fs-panel`) as one more host. It shall have no stacking level of its own and no bottom-bar handling of its own; the focus trap, Escape, the body scroll lock, the collapse on leaving the tab and the safe-area insets come from the hook and the class and are not rebuilt here. The tab's panel is wrapped in `MapTabShowingProvider` in `App.tsx`, and the opener registers through the hook's `registerToggle`. **Dependency:** removing the phone's bottom bar while a panel is expanded is the companion map build's change (`map-scroll-on-phones`, D-18 and D-19), not this build's; until the two are brought together (FR-90) the tree's full screen on a phone has whatever the maps' has. Open Question 19 holds what is still open.

## Non-Functional Requirements

> **NFR-01, Performance (derivation):** Building the model from the parsed files shall be linear in rows: a bounded number of passes over the parsed observations and over the parsed ML rows, each row visited a constant number of times, no nested loop over rows, no re-scan of raw file text. CI gates this as a work count (rows visited per derivation against rows present) over generated corpora of N and 10N rows. Any timing row runs only in the local full suite behind `SR_SKIP_LOCAL_ONLY_TIMING`, reading CPU time through `frontend/src/test/cpuTiming.ts`.

> **NFR-02, Responsiveness at the cap:** Opening the tab on a backup at the largest per-platform upload cap (read from `EBIRD_MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES_BY_PLATFORM` when the test runs, never typed in) shall not freeze the app: the file derivation shall run off the main thread under the house worker contracts (`parseOffThread` or `createStatsSession`), or on the main thread with a bound the schema states from a measurement at that cap. It never runs in a render body, and the tab shows its loading state and stays operable while it runs.

> **NFR-03, Control cost:** A control change shall not re-read or re-parse a stored file, and shall do work bounded linearly by the parsed rows with the constant stated in the schema and held by a work-count guard. A change of the provenance snapshot shall re-run only the escapee filter and the aggregation over species, never the file passes: every O(rows) structure this tab derives is keyed on its own inputs, not on the snapshot. This requirement covers the tab's own passes. The shared passive reader's confirmation pass (FR-20), which runs once over the observations on a snapshot change for each mounted reader, is the existing Life Lists behavior and is outside it; the feature adds no other work keyed on the snapshot. Reversal, as D-08 records it: if that pass is to be held to this requirement too, the tab takes the indexed confirmation shape Hotspot Reports uses, proven equal to the reader over a generated corpus.

> **NFR-04, Diagram scale:** The number of diagram elements mounted at once shall be bounded by one constant, independent of roster size: 600, clamped inside the function that decides what is mounted (D-10), with the guard reading the constant and never restating it. The constant moves only with a new measurement. The bound is held by a guard that builds a roster of every species in the bundled asset (read when the test runs). At that roster the arrival state, a full expand, zoom and pan each stay within the bound, and every species remains reachable through expand, zoom and find.

> **NFR-05, Memory and storage:** The model is in memory only. It holds no reference that outlives the loaded files (a new files epoch releases the previous model), copies neither the observations nor the ML rows, and adds no durable store: `clearDerived.ts` and `cacheInventory.test.ts` are unchanged. The only writes are the settings FR-32 names (Region, view and Names) and the rotate note's dismissal flag in the app's existing setting for it (FR-85), all through the `storage` seam.

> **NFR-06, Bundle:** Nothing lands on the entry chunk beyond the registry entries and the icon. The family asset, the tab and the model load with the tab; the diagram's renderer and any library it uses load only when the diagram is first shown and stay off the rollup's static graph. `entryChunk.test.ts` holds each.

> **NFR-07, Accessibility (rollup as equivalent):** The rollup is the diagram's non-graphical equivalent and shall carry every level (order, family, genus, species, Not placed), every count the diagram shows and every link the diagram offers. It shall meet WCAG 2.1 AA at 320 px wide and at 200% in-app text scale: every figure readable with no horizontal page scroll, every figure identified in text (never by column position alone once the layout reflows), no information carried by color alone.

> **NFR-08, Accessibility (controls):** Every app-owned button and link in the tab renders through the `Button` and `Link` primitives (or `OutboundLink`, `BirdName`, `ToggleSwitch`), with no raw `<button>` or `<a href>` and no unrostered `tabIndex` override; every select and text input has an explicit accessible name; dense controls meet the 44 px touch-target posture and no form control is below 16 px on a phone (`.sr-touch-target`, `.sr-input-16`); the headline sits in a polite live region that is always rendered, announces after the selection settles and uses the sequence-keyed child; focus is never dropped to the document body by a control this tab draws.

> **NFR-09, Theming and layout:** Every color is a `var(--sr-*)` token defined in both themes, with any text-on-fill pair (a seen-of-world bar, a media mark) verified at AA in both. Layout responds through classes in the established tiers, never inline `display` or widths. The diagram is contained by its own box so nothing it draws extends the page's scroll width. Any fixed or sticky element carries its `.sr-ios-app` safe-area rule and the `.sr-android-app` twin.

> **NFR-10, Security:** Classification comes from the bundled taxonomy and needs no new scan over raw file text. The requirements above do imply per-row work keyed on user-file fields, and the Architect's schema shall declare each one up front with its linearity argument, its own enforcement point and the `security.md` glob the holding module joins: the per-year species index (FR-19), the distinct-name count of FR-26, the per-family item tallies (FR-22), and the find match of FR-65, which is a scan over names that include user-file text (unplaced names) and shall be a plain bounded substring or prefix match with a capped query length and a capped read per name, never a regular expression built from the query, with its measured cost per keystroke stated. Passes reused unchanged from Life Lists are cited, not redeclared. Also: bundled-object lookups through `Object.hasOwn` or a `Map` (FR-24); ids keyed on an index (FR-45); every new href through its shape guard and `encodeURIComponent` (FR-50).

> **NFR-11, Compatibility:** The tab works on Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad, Android and the self-hosted web version. It uses no feature newer than Chromium 111 without a fallback (`ANDROID_WEBVIEW_FLOOR`), reads platform differences only through named predicates in `lib/platformGates.ts`, and any layout or gesture claim is confirmed in both WebKit and Chromium against the built app, since neither engine's result is inherited by the other.

> **NFR-12, Tab lifecycle:** Tabs stay mounted while hidden, so every page-level side effect the tab installs (a document or window listener for keys, wheel or pointer, a scroll lock, an observer) is active only while the tab is showing and is removed when it is not. The diagram's keys act only with focus inside it (FR-61).

> **NFR-13, Copy:** No em dash (U+2014) in any string the tab renders or in its Help section; American spelling; the serial comma; "weather" lowercase if it appears; tab names taken from `TAB_LABELS`. Count-bearing strings live in the copy module and are held by a rule-based sweep over a generated corpus (counts of zero, one and many through the shipped builders), not a ban list.

> **NFR-14, Verification method:** Reconciliation (FR-21) and the sum invariants (FR-39) are asserted structurally, as output equality against Life Lists' own functions and as identities over generated corpora, never as typed-in totals. A guard over the asset reads its figures from the asset when the test runs. Help, the changelog and pipeline records state properties, not counts.

## Out of Scope

From the strategic brief, unchanged:

- Unseen birds as tree content: no world tree, no ghosted unseen species, no list of families never seen. Unseen species exist only as each family's and order's denominator and in the headline totals. (Listing unseen families goes to the idea inbox.)
- Regional denominators ("seen of the species on the California list").
- Phylogenetic meaning: branch lengths, divergence dates, cladograms within a family.
- Subspecies and forms as their own leaves; spuhs, slashes and hybrids on the tree; a Count all forms mode.
- Arbitrary date ranges, protocol, breeding-code, age and sex filters; saved filter sets.
- Exporting or sharing the tree as an image or file; printing.
- A Statistics card for families, and "show in tree" links from other tabs or from Search results.
- Any new third-party request, endpoint, host, embedded page or durable store; any change to the escapee check or to who may start it.
- Editing, annotating or reclassifying anything.

Added while writing this PRD:

- An Include Exotics switch. Naturalized and provisional exotics are always counted, as at Life Lists' default.
- A filter that narrows both views to one order, family or genus. Focus in the diagram and find are view actions and change no count (Open Question 11).
- Genus denominators ("seen of the world's species in this genus").
- Placing a renamed, split or lumped name through the taxonomy history; such a name is Not placed (Open Question 14).
- Paging inside an opened family. (An expand-all control in the rollup was out of scope here until the design review asked for it; it is now FR-84.)
- Any change to `lib/useMapFullscreen.ts`, `.sr-map-fs-panel`, the full-screen panel's stacking level or the phone bottom bar's behavior while a panel is expanded. Those belong to the companion map build (D-18, D-19); this build consumes them (FR-89). (A full-screen mode for the diagram was out of scope here until the design review asked for it; it is now FR-87 to FR-89.)
- A tree that flows with the page on a phone. It was offered at the design review and not taken (D-20), and it would change the windowing of D-09 and D-10.
- A common name for an order or a genus from any source other than FR-82 and FR-83: no hand-authored genus names, no guessed names, no name read from the user's files or fetched.
- A Names choice per view or per level. It is one control for the whole tab.
- A backend twin of the family asset or any backend route.
- Persisting Year, Media, Include Escapees, sort, zoom, pan or expansion across launches, or syncing any of this tab's choices beyond what the `storage` seam already does for a setting.
- Outside links for orders and genera.
- Writing any published copy; the build prepares held proposals only (FR-74).

## Open Questions

Each has a default the build proceeds on unless it is answered before Stage 5. Questions 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11 and 12 are the Designer's, with the user at Stage 4; the requirements hold whichever way they go.

1. **Is the Birds of the World family URL shape real and stable?** The believed shape is the species shape with a family code. Cornell's pages refuse scripts, so this needs a person's look in a browser at two or three families. Default: no family link ships (FR-47); the guard, the builder and its runtime row may be built behind the verified flag.
2. **Does the Macaulay catalog accept a family code as `taxonCode` for the birder's own media?** Same check, in a browser. Default: no link ships (FR-48).
3. **For the Architect: does eBird's taxonomy response mark extinct species, and with which fields?** Answered (D-02): yes. An extinct taxon carries `extinct: true` with a numeric `extinctYear`, and both keys are absent otherwise. FR-04's extinct clause stands, with the family and order totals as amended there. A later response that drops the marker stops the generator, and the question returns here.
4. **The tab's label, key and icon.** Settled (D-24): the label is **Family Tree**, the user's own words in giving D-13; the key is `family-tree`; the icon is the design spec's. Reversal: the user says otherwise at the live look at the built app, and it is one label.
5. **Where the tab sits in the default order.** Closed by the user's direction (D-13): between Breeding Codes and Hotspot Reports (FR-08). The first default, directly after Life Lists, is withdrawn.
6. **Which view opens first, and whether a phone differs.** Closed (D-35, correcting D-25): at first use the tree when the window is wider than the still tier and the table at or under it; after the first change of view the choice is remembered on every tier (FR-53). This is the brief's original lean, rollup first on narrow screens. Reversal: one default.
7. **The shape of the Media filter and the Year control.** Default: three per-kind controls each with no constraint, has, lacks (FR-30), and a select of the years present (FR-29).
8. **Are media figures scoped by Region and Year, or always the species' media anywhere?** Settled as the default below (D-24): media figures follow Region and Year like every other figure on the tab, so FR-22 stands unchanged. Reversal: the user says otherwise at the live look. The original default and its reasoning: scoped (FR-22), because it is the only rule under which "has a photo" in a county equals Life Lists' county photo list and each family's media column equals what its filter selects. The cost is that a leaf's media marks under a region can differ from Life Lists' presence cell, which shows the species' media anywhere; Help says how media is scoped.
9. **The diagram's form, what is expanded on arrival, and how counts and media state sit on nodes.** Settled at the design review: the form and the node figures are the approved mockup's, which the design spec states, and the tree arrives showing birds by name at reading size from the top of the tree, with Fit one press away, not as a whole-tree overview (D-24). On the phone tier the in-page tree is a still (FR-87, D-20). The first default (orders and families shown, genera and species collapsed) is withdrawn. Everything still fits NFR-04. Reversal: the user says otherwise at the live look.
10. **What a plain wheel does over the diagram.** Default: a trackpad pinch and Ctrl or Cmd with the wheel zoom; a plain wheel pans the diagram while it can and otherwise scrolls the page, so the page is never trapped.
11. **What order and genus nodes do, having no outside page.** Default: expand or collapse, and focus (fit that branch in view). No filter that changes counts.
12. **The diagram's keyboard model.** Default: one tab stop, arrow keys along the tree, Enter or Space to act, `aria-activedescendant` (FR-68). The key bindings may change at design; the fixed tab-stop count and no pointer-only action may not.
13. **For the Architect: can each leaf host `<BirdName>` within the mounted bound?** Closed (D-11): the leaf is plain text and the active leaf's readout renders `<BirdName>`. The mounted bound would allow it on the leaf; the keyboard contract does not, because a diagram that is one tab stop with `aria-activedescendant` cannot hold focusable content inside a node, and `<BirdName>` renders a button and two links. The readout's tab stops are a fixed count independent of the roster, the number stated in the design spec (FR-68); D-11's "at most three" is superseded. The rollup renders every species through `<BirdName>`. Reversal: a keyboard model with focusable nodes, which FR-68 rules out.
14. **Should a name the current taxonomy does not list be placed through the taxonomy history?** Default: no. One roster entry stays one row, which is what keeps the totals equal to Life Lists; the name shows under Not placed with its reason.
15. **For the Architect: how is the first family asset generated against the committed snapshot outside the annual run?** Decided (D-03): the asset is written only by the existing generator, under its unchanged revision check, with no second entry point. That check stops the run today, because the live taxonomy has drifted from the committed snapshot, so it is raised and not worked around. The route is the user's choice and is pending: refresh the snapshot first by the annual runbook's steps (recommended), or a one-time bootstrap of this one asset from the saved response through the same derivation and checks, which is what this question's original default ruled out and so needs the user's explicit yes and its own decisions entry. Slice A's first step waits on that choice; nothing else in this PRD depends on it.
16. **Does Region share Life Lists' remembered region?** Default: no, its own setting key, so the two tabs are set independently.
17. **Is the diagram gated on any platform?** Default: no gate. A gate exists only as a measured, written decision (FR-71).
18. **Should the world family and order totals count every family and order, wholly extinct ones included?** Default: no (FR-04 as amended, D-05): a taxon with no living species counts only when the birder has a species of it. Reversal: the user prefers every family counted, which is a change of one predicate and of QA-36.
19. **OPEN, shared with the companion map build: is the bottom bar removed while a full-screen panel is expanded?** This build's requirement is only that the tree's full screen behaves as the maps' does (FR-89). The map build's proposed answer (the bar is removed while any panel is expanded, driven from the shared hook, the panel left at its present stacking level; fallback: the panels are raised above the bar and the bar made inert) is accepted here and awaits that build's own design pass (D-19). Default: this build proceeds on the proposed answer, builds nothing of its own for it, and QA-99's phone leg is judged on the combined tree. If the answer changes, this build follows it with no requirement change.
20. **Does Find match only the name the Names control shows, or both names?** Closed as assumed (D-25): Find matches both the common and the scientific name in every Names state; the control decides which name leads in a result and ranks first (FR-65).
21. **Does the readout follow the Names control?** Closed as assumed (D-25): the readout always shows both names, common name leading (FR-79).
22. **Does D-24's arrival decision change which view opens first?** Closed (D-35, correcting D-25): the first view follows the tier, the tree above the still tier and the table at or under it. FR-53, QA-51 and Open Question 6 state it.
23. **The genus naming rule's details.** Closed (D-25): the rule is written out in FR-83 (who qualifies, the whole last word before its part after the hyphen, the regular endings and the closed exception list, the three cases with no name), and the one-time read-through of the generator's world output for awkward plurals is the ship-set step FR-91.
