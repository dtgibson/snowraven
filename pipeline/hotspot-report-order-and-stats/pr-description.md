## Hotspot Reports: the life list last, and the place's figures first (hotspot-report-order-and-stats)

### What this does

Reorders a place's report on the Hotspot Reports tab so it opens on a summary of the place and ends on the long list: header, **At a glance**, the jump row (one column only), Map, Visits and media, Weather on your visits, Checklists by protocol, **Most-recorded species**, and the Life list last, at every width and in the DOM. At a glance is a new card of count tiles (Days birded, Years birded, Species per hour, Recorded once, Longest gap) over fact rows (first and latest visit, time birded with its average, distance traveled, most species on one checklist, first and newest species here), each stating its denominator; Most-recorded species ranks up to ten of the life list's own rows by how many of the place's checklists include each. Every species figure follows the tab's one species rule (FR-36); nothing new is requested, stored or switched.

### How to test

1. From `frontend/`: `npm run typecheck`, `npx eslint .`, `npm run build`, and the full suite `npx vitest run` (judge any timing-ratio row on a quiet machine; re-run such a file alone if it reads high under load).
2. The feature's own suites: `npx vitest run src/lib/hotspotReports src/components/hotspotReports src/lib/helpToc.test.ts`. The rows that carry this change: `hotspotReportsLocation.test.ts` (the opening figures held to what the life list implies, computed from the raw rows; the date figures held to a `Date.UTC` oracle; the distance bound at its edge; every thin-place rule), `hotspotReportsCopy.test.ts` (the new strings swept at 0, 1 and 2, and in all three date-format preferences), `HotspotReportsTab.test.tsx` (the reading order, the chips equal to the sections after the row, the tiles and facts as rendered, Most-recorded present exactly when it has something to rank), `hotspotReportsCss.test.ts` (rail first, no `order` or grid placement on a section, the two track minima), `hotspotReportsLinearity.test.ts` (two new rows and two new mutants), `hotspotReportsPublishedClaims.test.ts` (Help's reordered section).
3. The real-engine harness, against the built dist: `node website/tools/verify/verify-hotspot-reports.mjs frontend/dist` (fixed) and `... --expect-broken` (must print HARNESS DISCRIMINATES).
4. In the app with the demo backup: open Hotspot Reports, type "jamaica" in the picker and press Enter. The report opens with the header, then At a glance (tiles, then the facts, two across in a wide window), then the rail of Map, Visits and media, Weather, Protocols and Most-recorded species on the left with the life list on the right. Narrow the window, or set Text size to 200% in Settings: everything stacks in that order, the life list last, and the jump row sits under At a glance with one chip per section after it. Choose a place with one checklist: no Most-recorded section and no chip for it.

## Convention Flags

- **A section's many / one / none rules live in one pure view function beside its derivation** (`glanceView`, `mostRecordedView` in `lib/hotspotReportsLocation.ts`), and anything else that depends on whether the section exists (here the jump chips) reads the same view, so the rule is tested in the node suite and cannot disagree with the markup.
- **An em container threshold inside `.sr-panel` is reachable only while the report can be that wide under the panel's 1280px cap**: Hotspot Reports' 53.25em flips at 100% and 125% text and can never flip at 150% or 200%. A real-window probe states which shipped text scales can reach a threshold and asserts the fixed form at the others, rather than letting "never reached" pass as an absence of failures.
