# PRD: Hotspot Reports
**Feature:** hotspot-reports
**Date:** 2026-11-10
**Stage:** 2, The Planner
**Source:** strategic-brief.md (approved)

## Feature Overview

A new **Hotspot Reports** tab (`hotspot-reports`) that reports on any one location in the user's own eBird backup, a public eBird hotspot or a personal location, chosen from a searchable dropdown. Before a choice it shows a small set of general statistics about the user's locations; after one it shows that location's life list, a map, its checklists by eBird protocol, the weather on the user's visits there, and a fixed short set of other statistics, all derived from the two files already loaded.

Requirement IDs below are this feature's own. Where a requirement of a shipped tab is meant it is cited by its feature folder (for example `life-lists-tab FR-11`). Default wordings are given so two engineers build the same thing; the Designer may restyle a default wording but not re-source what it states.

**Amended 2026-11-10** as a scoped errand after the schema and the design review, with every ID kept and new QA rows added at the end of the table. It folds in: the user's directions at the design review (the tab's position between Breeding Codes and Checklists; the first design direction accepted as drawn; `decisions.md` D-01 to D-03); and the conflicts the Architect settled in `schema.md` (the picker window, the escapee confirmation as a result, the years bound, and three readings this document had left open). **Amended again 2026-11-11** after the build, QA and security review, so the document does not trail the built tab: FR-15 and FR-16 agree on a location with no region code (D-04), QA-33's example is a name eBird publishes (D-05), and NFR-03 states Show all as the exception to the life list's page bound (D-23).

## User Stories

> **US-01** As a birder with a home patch, I want to pick that place and see my own record there (which species, first seen when, on how many of my checklists), so that I have a life list for one location, which eBird does not give me for a personal location at all.

> **US-02** As a birder with many locations, I want to find a place by typing part of its name and to tell apart two places that share a name, so that I open the report I meant.

> **US-03** As a birder opening the tab with nothing chosen, I want a short overview of my locations (how many, how many are hotspots, where I bird most and where I have the most species) whose rows open that place, so that the page is useful before I search.

> **US-04** As a birder, I want to see what kinds of checklists I submitted at a place and what the weather was on my visits, with the page saying plainly how much of my record each figure rests on, so that I do not read a number built on three checklists as a finding.

> **US-05** As a birder without an eBird key, offline, or rate-limited, I want every location still offered and reportable, with a location simply carrying no kind until eBird has answered, so that a place is never called personal because nothing has been heard.

> **US-06** As a birder who also uses Statistics and the Map Explorer, I want the page to say which counting rule its species figure follows, so that a different number for the same place on another tab is explained rather than looking like a bug.

> **US-07** As a keyboard or screen reader user on a phone-width screen at large text, I want the picker, the overview rows and the report to be fully operable and announced, so that the tab works for me as the rest of the app does.

## Functional Requirements

### The tab and its place in the navigation

> **FR-01** The app shall add one configurable tab with id `hotspot-reports` and label **Hotspot Reports** in `TAB_LABELS` (`frontend/src/lib/tabLayout.ts`), rendered in its own panel and loaded lazily like every other tab. Every user-facing mention of the tab (navigation, tab-layout settings, Search, Help, in-app copy) shall use that one label. Inside the page the copy shall use eBird's terms, "hotspot" and "personal location". The Multimedia tab (`life-list`), the Life Lists tab (`life-lists`) and the Map Explorer's Hotspots view keep their ids, labels and behavior.

> **FR-02** `DEFAULT_TAB_ORDER` shall be the order that shipped with Life Lists (the twelve ids in today's constant) with `hotspot-reports` inserted between `breeding-codes` and `checklists` (the user's direction at the design review, 2026-11-10; `decisions.md` D-03), every other id keeping its relative order: `weather`, `birding-stats`, `map-explorer`, `species-detail`, `calendar`, `targets`, `life-list`, `breeding-codes`, `hotspot-reports`, `checklists`, `life-lists`, `comparer`, `named-birds`. The position is outside the phone bar's default first four, which are unchanged.

> **FR-03** The tab-layout default shall migrate at read time exactly as the Life Lists tab's did (`parseLayout`, `PREVIOUS_DEFAULT_TAB_ORDER`): `PREVIOUS_DEFAULT_TAB_ORDER` becomes, as a spelled-out literal, the twelve-id default that FR-02 replaces, and the 1.0.38 order it holds today is retired. A saved order that, after the unknown-id drop and the missing-tab append, equals that previous default given the same append shall read as the new default, so Hotspot Reports sits between Breeding Codes and Checklists for that user. Any other saved order shall be kept as saved with `hotspot-reports` appended last and visible. The hidden set is preserved in both cases, nothing is written back by the read, and exactly one generation migrates (a saved 1.0.38 default is treated as a custom order).

> **FR-04** The tab shall have its own glyph in `TAB_ICONS` (`frontend/src/lib/tabIcons.tsx`), distinguishable from every other destination's glyph at the icon-rail size; shall appear as a destination in Search (the command palette) under its label; and shall appear in Settings' tab-layout controls (reorder, hide, show, Restore defaults) with no tab-specific code path.

> **FR-05** With thirteen tabs, the phone More sheet shall be tall enough that every destination it lists (the tabs outside the bar's four, and Settings) is visible without scrolling the sheet, at the default text size on the 393 by 852 reference phone (WebKit, `.sr-ios-app`, a 34px bottom inset), so all thirteen tabs are reachable from the bar and the open sheet with nothing to scroll. That property is the requirement, not a pixel constant: the sheet's cap (drawn in the mockup as `min(73dvh, 616px)`) and the number `TabNav.test.tsx` pins (528 today) are set from the built app's WebKit measurement, which governs over the mockup's figure, and move together. Settings stays pinned in the sheet's foot at every size; on a shorter screen the list may scroll as it does today (`.claude/rules/ui.md`, v1.0.45).

> **FR-06** The tab shall mount on first open and stay mounted while hidden, as every deferred tab does, and shall put the data-file epoch (`useFilesEpoch`) in its load effect's dependencies.

### Data states

> **FR-07** With no eBird backup stored, the tab shall show the house setup guidance for the backup (the steps and the route to Settings the Life Lists tab shows) and no picker, statistics or map. A file-status lookup that fails is unknown, never empty: it shall render the shared load-failure state through `TabLoadErrorAlert`, mounted in every branch that can reach the error phase, and never the setup guidance.

> **FR-08** With a backup that yields no location (FR-10 leaves nothing, including a backup with no rows), the tab shall show one sentence saying the backup holds no locations to report on (default "Your eBird backup has no locations to report on."), the picker rendered natively disabled with the overview choice shown, and no count block, ranked list or map.

> **FR-09** The Macaulay Library export is optional. With it absent, or present but unreadable, everything except the media counts (FR-55) shall render normally.

### Locations and checklists

> **FR-10** A location is one distinct **Location ID** cell of the loaded backup, compared exactly as parsed, held as a `Map` key and never as an object key. A Location ID that is empty, or longer than the stored id bound the app already uses for a location id (16 characters, `HOTSPOT_ID_MAX_CHARS`), names no location. Which location a row counts at is decided by its checklist's first row (FR-11), so a checklist whose first row carries such an id is offered nowhere and counted in no figure on this tab. No other source (eBird's API, the ML export, a hotspot list) shall supply a location, so every offered location has at least one checklist in the backup.

> **FR-11** A checklist is one distinct non-empty **Submission ID**, rolled up over all of the backup's observations (not only the countable ones) with the meaning of `computeChecklists`: its location, name, place cells, date, time, protocol, duration, All Obs Reported value and comments are those of its first observation in file order. An observation follows its checklist's location, with the checklist's first row in file order deciding: a later row of a located checklist counts at that location even when its own Location ID is blank, over the bound or different, and every row of a checklist whose first row has no usable Location ID counts nowhere. The backup does not disagree within one checklist; a test row pins both directions. A row with an empty Submission ID is ignored by every figure on this tab.

> **FR-12** A location's name, County and State/Province are those of its first checklist in file order. Its coordinates are those of its first checklist in file order whose latitude is finite and within -90 to 90 and whose longitude is finite and within -180 to 180; a location with no such checklist has no coordinates. Its place label is the Checklists place form "County, ST" (`placeOf`), empty when neither cell yields one.

> **FR-13** A location name shall be read and shown through one length bound (a named constant, 200 characters): a longer name is shown cut at the bound with a written ellipsis, and matching (FR-19) reads only the bounded text. A location whose name is empty shall be shown under a stated placeholder (default "Unnamed location"). Both are still offered and reportable, because identity is the Location ID.

> **FR-14** Two locations that share a name are two locations everywhere on the tab: two picker rows, two possible rows in a ranked list, two reports, with no figure merged across them.

### Hotspot or personal: the kind of a location

> **FR-15** Each location shall have exactly one of three kinds, read from the shared hotspot resolution the app's hotspot links already use (`lib/hotspotSet.ts`: the Set and its per-region state): a location whose State/Province cell is not an askable region code is **not yet known**, whatever the Set holds; otherwise it is a **hotspot** when its id is in the Set (`isPublicHotspot`), **personal** when it is not in the Set and its region's state is answered, and **not yet known** in every other case. The property that governs is equality with the shipped share: the kinds, counted over the locations, equal `computeHotspotShare` run over one row per location rather than one per checklist, so the region code is read first, as that function reads it (`decisions.md` D-04).

> **FR-16** A location shall never be shown, counted or announced as personal because eBird has not answered. Each of these leaves a location with an askable region code not yet known unless its id is already in the Set: the region's first lookup pending; no eBird key; offline before a first answer; a region eBird refused past the Set's retry rounds; a failed lookup. A location with no askable State/Province code is not yet known in every case, in the Set or not (FR-15), so it carries no kind and, by FR-32, no hotspot link, and is never called personal; a State/Province cell longer than 16 characters is read as no region code. No eBird export produces a listed hotspot with no region code, so this corner changes nothing a user sees and errs toward saying less.

> **FR-17** The tab shall read the kind only through the shared Set (the `useHotspotSet` family and `getHotspotResolution`). It adds no request of its own, no retry control and no second classification; the Set's own schedule, storage and invalidation apply unchanged. When the Set changes, every kind on the page (picker rows, the count block, ranked rows, the report header) shall update without a reload. No part of a report other than the kind mark and the hotspot link depends on the classification.

### The location picker

> **FR-18** The tab shall show one location picker, present in both the overview and a report, through which exactly one location, or the overview, is selected at a time. Its first row is always the overview choice (default wording "Overview"); after it come exactly the locations of FR-10, one row each.

> **FR-19** Typing shall filter the rows by one case-insensitive substring test of the trimmed query against each location's bounded name, its place label and, where the app knows it, the full state or province name (`regionName`), the same matching the Targets county picker and the Life Lists Region picker use. There is no fuzzy matching and no accent folding. An empty query matches every location. A query matching nothing shows the house no-match line (default "No location matches."). The query input accepts at most 100 characters.

> **FR-20** Rows shall be ordered by name (locale compare, as the Region picker orders its labels), then by place label, then by Location ID in code-unit order, with unnamed locations after named ones. The order shall not depend on the kind, so rows never move when the classification arrives.

> **FR-21** Each row shall show the location's name, its place label when it has one, and its kind in three states: a hotspot mark, a personal-location mark, or no mark at all when the kind is not yet known (no "unknown" word). The kind shall be carried in text available to assistive technology, never by color alone. Where two or more locations share both name and place label, each of those rows shall also show its Location ID, so namesakes are always distinguishable. A per-row checklist count is optional and, if the Designer adds it, comes from the same derivation.

> **FR-22** The open list shall render at most a fixed number of location options (a named constant the file cannot change, 200), as one window of consecutive matches in FR-20's order. With a typed query the window is the first matches. With an empty query the window is the first 200 locations, except that when the selected location sorts beyond them the window slides so that location's row is rendered and can be the active row on open (FR-23); typing returns to the first matches. When more locations match than are rendered, one non-selectable line shall say so and ask the user to type to narrow (default "Showing 200 of N locations. Type to narrow."), reading the same wherever the window sits; the arrow keys never land on it.

> **FR-23** The picker shall be a member of the house combobox family (`TargetsCountyPicker`, `LifeListsRegionPicker`): the input is the combobox and one tab stop, the chevron `Button` the other, options are plain `role="option"` elements with no tab stop and no per-option handler, the active option is carried by `aria-activedescendant`, and every id or IDREF is keyed on an index, never on a name or a Location ID. Keyboard behavior shall equal the Region picker's: focus opens the list with the selected row active, wherever that location sorts (FR-22's window makes it a rendered row); typing filters and makes the first location row active; ArrowDown and ArrowUp move and stop at the ends; Home and End jump; Enter chooses the active row; Escape closes the list and is consumed only while it is open; Tab closes the list and moves on; a press outside closes it.

> **FR-24** Choosing a row, by Enter or by pointer, shall close the list, leave keyboard focus in the picker, show the chosen location's bounded name (or the overview wording) as the picker's value, and replace the page content: a location shows its report, the overview choice shows the general statistics.

> **FR-25** On every change of what is selected, by the picker or by a ranked row, one always-mounted polite live region shall announce the result once: for a location, its name with its species and checklist totals (default "{name}: {S} species, {C} checklists."); for the overview, the location total (default "Overview: {L} locations.").

### General statistics, shown before a choice

> **FR-26** With a backup loaded and no location selected, the page shall show the picker and exactly this: the count block of FR-27 and the two ranked lists of FR-28 and FR-29. It shall show no map, no chart and no other statistic. The only control in the count block is the house link to Settings that the no-key reason carries (FR-27).

> **FR-27** The count block shall always state the number of locations in the backup. Its split has three states (`decisions.md` D-02). (1) **Every location classified:** the hotspot figure and the personal figure, summing to the total, with no third figure and no reason. (2) **Some classified, some not:** the hotspot figure, the personal figure and the not-yet-known figure, the three summing to the total, with one reason sentence; the known figures are shown whatever the reason, including with no key when stored answers already classify some locations. (3) **No location classified** (at least one location is not yet known and no location is a hotspot or personal, for example no eBird key and nothing stored): the split is not drawn and no zero is printed for hotspots or personal locations, because a zero would read as a finding; the total stands with one sentence saying the kinds are not yet known, and the reason. The reason is chosen in this order (the Statistics hotspot share's precedence, applied only when a location is not yet known): no eBird key; still checking (the key status unknown or a region pending); offline; eBird has not answered for some regions; otherwise locations with no region code. The no-key reason carries the house link to Settings (default "Add a key in Settings", the Statistics hotspot block's own control).

> **FR-28** **Most checklists**: the ten locations with the most checklists (FR-11), in descending order; ties broken by species total (FR-36) descending, then name by locale compare, then Location ID in code-unit order. Each row shows rank, bounded name, place label, kind mark where known, and the checklist count.

> **FR-29** **Most species**: the ten locations with the highest species total under the tab's one species rule (FR-36), in descending order; ties broken by checklist count descending, then name, then Location ID. A location whose species total is zero is never listed. Each row shows rank, bounded name, place label, kind mark where known, and the species total.

> **FR-30** Each ranked row shall be one `Button` whose press selects that location exactly as the picker would (FR-24, FR-25), with an accessible name that contains the row's visible name and figure. After a row press keyboard focus shall land on the report's name heading (`decisions.md` D-01; not the picker, because focus on the picker opens its list), and never on the document body. With exactly one location in the backup each list is replaced by one sentence naming that location with a control that opens its report; a list with no qualifying location renders nothing, heading included.

### The report for one location

> **FR-31** With a location selected the page shall show the picker and that location's report, made of exactly these parts, in this reading order at every width: header (FR-32, carrying the totals of FR-50 and the rule sentence of FR-39), life list (FR-33 to FR-38), map (FR-40, FR-41), checklists by protocol (FR-42 to FR-44), weather (FR-45 to FR-49), other statistics (FR-51 to FR-56). The report is two columns where its own width allows and one column otherwise, which is always the case on a phone. While it is one column, a jump row of section chips, one per section below the header, sits under the header; a chip scrolls its section into view and moves keyboard focus to that section's heading. The jump row is navigation: it filters, switches and hides nothing, is absent while the report is two columns, and is not one of the controls FR-56 excludes.

> **FR-32** The header shall show the location's bounded name, its kind where known, its county and state/province in the app's existing display forms, and, only when the location is a hotspot, the link to its eBird hotspot page through `HotspotLink` (which also refuses an id that is not `L` plus digits). A personal or not-yet-known location's name is plain text.

### The life list at the location, and the tab's one species rule

> **FR-33** A species is on a location's life list exactly when the location holds at least one observation of it that can establish a first sighting under the Life Lists tab's rule (`life-lists-tab FR-10 to FR-12`): a valid `YYYY-MM-DD` date, a countable form by `isNonCountableForm` on the name as exported, the name then normalized so a form folds into its species, and a non-empty Submission ID; and the species is not left out by FR-35.

> **FR-34** Each row shall be dated to the species' earliest such observation at that location, under the Life Lists order (day, then time with a blank time last, then Submission ID; `life-lists-tab FR-11`), and shall carry that observation's checklist link through `ChecklistLink`. A species first seen elsewhere shall never show its global first date, checklist or place in a location's list.

> **FR-35** Exotics and escapees are treated as the Life Lists tab treats them at its defaults, with no switch on this tab. A species is left out exactly when its name is in the confirmed escapee set: the names in the backup that Statistics' exotic status check has published as escapee-only and that the app's existing confirmation honors against the loaded backup, which is the result Life Lists and Species Detail get from `confirmPublishedNames`. The requirement is that result, not that function: the tab may compute the set from its own index, provided a test proves the indexed result equal to the shipped confirmation's, restricted to names the backup holds, over a generated corpus whose first rows are the failure returns (nothing published, nothing consulted, a store that failed to load). Naturalized and provisional exotics are listed; the published exotic list is not read by this tab. The classification is read passively: the tab never starts, retries or extends the check, and a species the check has not settled, or any species when nothing has been published, is listed.

> **FR-36** One species rule governs every species figure on the tab: a location's **species total** is the number of rows on its life list (FR-33 to FR-35). The report's headline species total, the most-species ranking (FR-29), the tie-breaks in FR-28, the announcement of FR-25 and the per-checklist figure of FR-54 all use it, so no two figures on the tab can disagree about one location.

> **FR-37** Each life-list row shall show: the species through `<BirdName>` (linking to Species Detail, since every listed species is in the backup), the first date at the location, that checklist's link, and the number of the location's checklists the species is on (distinct checklists at the location carrying at least one countable-form observation that normalizes to the species, dated or not). Rows are ordered newest first by first date at the location, in the Life Lists display order (`life-lists-tab FR-28`), and paged as the Life Lists tab pages (100 rows, then its show-more controls). The life list is a list, not a table. At wide widths it carries a visual column header row that is hidden from assistive technology; at every width each cell carries its own words for assistive technology (for example "First recorded here" before the date, and "On 212 of 412 checklists here"); and the stacked narrow form spells those words out on screen. The header row is the one logged deviation from the Life Lists ledger pattern, which has no column header (`decisions.md` D-01).

> **FR-38** A location whose life list is empty (every species there is a non-countable form, or every species is left out by FR-35) is still offered and reportable: the list is replaced by one sentence (default "No countable species at this location yet."), the species total reads 0, and every other part of the report renders.

> **FR-39** The report shall carry one short sentence stating the rule, always visible and placed directly under the totals in the report header (FR-50), because the page headlines a figure a user can compare with two other tabs. Default: "Counted the way Life Lists counts: subspecies fold into their species; spuhs, slashes and hybrids are left out, and so are species the exotic status check on Statistics found to be escapees. Statistics and Map Explorer count places by their own rules, so their numbers for this place can differ."

### The map

> **FR-40** When the selected location has coordinates (FR-12) the report shall show a map of it through `<SnowMap>` with one marker at those coordinates and an accessible name that names the location. It is a plain map: no popup and no corner controls (no share, fullscreen or location button), only the zoom control and attribution every `<SnowMap>` draws. The map shall be mounted only then: the overview, and a report for a location without coordinates, mount no map and load no map tile. The map carries the same gesture posture as the app's other in-page maps.

> **FR-41** When the selected location has no coordinates the map shall be replaced by one plain sentence (default "Your backup has no coordinates for this location, so there is no map.").

### Checklists by protocol

> **FR-42** The report shall count the location's checklists by the checklist's Protocol cell, named through `protocolName()` as the Checklists tab names them. Protocol cells that share a name after naming are counted in one row under that name (`schema.md` section 4.3). A checklist whose Protocol cell is blank shall be counted in a stated bucket (default "No protocol recorded"), never dropped. The counts shall sum to the location's checklist total.

> **FR-43** Protocol rows shall be ordered by count descending, then by name. At most ten named protocols are shown; any further ones are summed into one row (default "Other protocols") so the sum of FR-42 still holds, with the blank bucket last. A protocol name is shown through a length bound (a named constant, 64 characters).

> **FR-44** The report shall state the complete-checklist share: of the location's checklists whose All Obs Reported value is known, how many are complete, with both counts shown and the percentage through `fmtSharePct`. When some checklists do not say, that count is stated. When none says, the share is replaced by a sentence.

### Weather on the user's visits

> **FR-45** Weather shall be reported only from the weather blocks already in the comments of the location's own checklists, with the meaning of `computeWeatherStats` applied to that location's checklists: a block is found by the existing attribution gate and is readable when the existing parser returns at least one field. No lookup, request, key or stored document is used, and none is offered to fill a gap.

> **FR-46** Whenever the location has at least one found block, the section shall state its coverage: how many of the location's checklists carry a readable block out of the location's checklist total, and, when any found block is unreadable, how many.

> **FR-47** The floor is the app's existing per-subject weather floor, `WEATHER_SPECIES_MIN_CHECKLISTS` (10), read from that one constant and not restated. With fewer readable-block checklists than the floor the section shall show the coverage line and one sentence saying that is too few to summarize, and no distribution or typical value.

> **FR-48** At or above the floor the section shall show three figures, each over the app's existing bands and each with its own denominator stated (a readable block can carry one field and not another): **sky condition** over the eleven conditions, **temperature** over the seven temperature bands, **wind** over the nine Beaufort bands. For each, the distribution of the location's readable-block checklists across the bands and a typical value, which is the band with the most checklists, named in words above its group (default "Most common: {band}") and never marked by color alone. A tie goes to the band the section displays first: the app's sky-condition display order for sky, ascending band order for temperature and wind. A figure whose own denominator is below the floor shows its counts and, in place of the typical value, a sentence saying there are too few to name one.

> **FR-49** With no found block at the location the section shall show one sentence saying so (default "None of your checklists here carries a weather block."), and nothing else.

### Other statistics

> **FR-50** **Totals**: the location's checklist total (FR-11) and species total (FR-36), shown as the headline pair in the report header with the rule sentence of FR-39 directly under them, and not repeated among the other statistics.

> **FR-51** **First and latest visit**: the location's earliest and latest checklists that carry a valid date, under the Life Lists instant order (day, then time with a blank time last, then Submission ID), each with its date and its checklist link. A location with one dated checklist shows one line, labeled "Only visit", not two. A location with no dated checklist shows a sentence instead.

> **FR-52** **Visits by month and by year**: the count of the location's dated checklists in each of the twelve calendar months, all twelve present even at zero; and the count in each calendar year that has at least one, in ascending order (only years present). At most the 100 most recent such years are shown as rows; any older years are summed into one leading row labeled "Before {first shown year}", so the year figures still sum to the dated checklists and a file of bad dates cannot lengthen the list. When some of the location's checklists carry no valid date, the figure states how many it rests on out of the total.

> **FR-53** **Time birded**: the sum of the durations of the location's checklists that carry a usable duration, where usable is the Statistics duration rule (`computeDurationBins`: not blank, not negative, at most 1,440 minutes), shown in hours and minutes with its denominator stated ("over N of M checklists that recorded a duration"). When no checklist recorded one, a sentence says so and no total is shown.

> **FR-54** **The checklist with the most species**: the location's checklist with the highest number of species under FR-36's rule, the earliest by FR-51's order winning a tie, shown with its species count, date and checklist link. Omitted when no checklist at the location has a countable species.

> **FR-55** **Media**: the number of photos, audio recordings and videos in the loaded ML export whose checklist id is non-empty and equals the Submission ID of a checklist at the location. The ML export's own locality, county, latitude and longitude never decide. With the ML export absent the line shall say the counts need the Macaulay Library export; with it present but unreadable the line shall say it could not be read; neither shall read as zero. With it loaded, a true zero reads as zero.

> **FR-56** The report shall contain no statistic, filter, switch, gallery, tide figure, breeding-code or named-bird section beyond FR-32 to FR-55.

### Re-derivation and persistence

> **FR-57** Every structure derived from the files shall be re-derived when the data-file epoch changes (a save, replace or remove of either file by any writer, an iCloud arrival or clear included) and not on a picker press. If the selected location is still in the backup its report re-derives in place; if it is not, the page falls back to the general statistics silently, with no message, and the selection is dropped.

> **FR-58** The tab shall persist nothing: the selected location lives for the session only, no setting, document or cache is written, nothing is added to iCloud Sync, and no row is added to `clearDerived.ts`.

### Help, published copy and release

> **FR-59** `docs/HELP.md` shall gain a `## Hotspot Reports` section and its Help table of contents entry in the same change, placed where the tab sits in the default order (between Breeding Codes and Checklists), with the tab's name added wherever Help lists the tabs. It shall cover: what a location is and that only places in the backup are offered; the picker and how namesakes are told apart; the three kinds and why a kind can be missing; the overview; each part of the report with its denominators; the weather source, coverage and floor; that media counts follow the linked checklist; that nothing is remembered between launches; and the species rule with a plain account of why Statistics' top locations by species and the Map Explorer's My species can show a different number for the same place (the three differences recorded in Out of Scope, "Figures left as they are"). Every existing Help sentence about the app's maps shall be kept true. ## Non-Functional Requirements

> **NFR-01, Network (the durable form):** The feature adds no third-party request, no new endpoint or host, and no request moved between components. Requests a system component makes from the app's process count toward this statement and the feature adds none: no iframe and no embedded media. What it reuses, each at its existing enforcement point and unchanged in shape: (a) the map tiles and style `<SnowMap>` already requests from the existing keyless tile hosts, made only after a location with coordinates is selected, for the area around that location; (b) the existing `/map/hotspot-region` lookup behind the shared hotspot Set, with the user's own eBird key, its existing gate and its existing 30-day store, which this tab may be the first surface in a session to trigger and which asks nothing when every region is stored and fresh; (c) the existing batched `/taxonomy/codes` post that every tab drawing `<BirdName>` makes, at most once per change in the set of names a report shows and never on the overview; (d) the bird-link favicon images `<BirdName>` already loads from hosts already in the Tauri content security policy; (e) outbound links the user presses. On web/Pi the loaded files, the key status and the lazy chunks reach the page over HTTP from the user's own server as today. `CACHED_GET_PATHS`, `EBIRD_GATED_PATHS`, the Vite proxy, `app.security.csp`, `http:allow-fetch`, the backend routes and the `lib/tauri/` services are unchanged.

> **NFR-02, Security (declared scans over user-file text):** The feature adds these reads over user-file text, and the schema shall declare each with its `security.md` citation, a linearity argument, and its measured cost per draw and per re-draw at the 150,000-row upload cap: (1) one pass over the observations building the per-location index, once per files epoch; (2) the per-keystroke filter over location names in the picker; (3) the weather-block parse over the selected location's checklists, per selection; and (4) the pass joining ML rows to checklists, once per epoch. Each shall have a linearity row in a test suite, timed through `frontend/src/test/cpuTiming.ts`, with a non-vacuity mutant, over at least these hostile shapes: every row its own location; one location holding every row; every checklist of one location carrying a weather block. No pass is superlinear in rows, and a pass that sorts derives its limit from n log n as `lifeListsLinearity.test.ts` does.

> **NFR-03, Security (bounds the file cannot inflate):** Every allocation sized by a count read from the file carries a second bound the content cannot raise: locations, checklists and (location, species) pairs are each at most the observation count, which the upload cap bounds. Rendered output is bounded independently of the file: the picker at 200 options (FR-22), each ranked list at ten, protocol rows at ten named rows plus the other and blank rows (FR-43), months at twelve, years at the 100 most recent with older years summed into one row labeled "Before {first shown year}" (FR-52), the paged life list at its page size (100 rows), a location name at 200 characters and a protocol name at 64. **Show all is the stated exception:** it renders every row of the location's life list, so its rendered rows are bounded by that location's species total (itself at most the observation count), not by a constant. Its measured cost at a 3,000-species location is 0.54 to 0.60 s in Chromium and 1.0 s to commit in WebKit, the same class as the Life Lists tab's own Show all on the same data, and no real location holds that many species (`decisions.md` D-23). The schema shall state each bound beside the structure it bounds.

> **NFR-04, Memory:** The per-location index and everything derived from it shall live only as long as the loaded file it was built from. No module-scoped holder keyed on file content exists without a teardown; a replace or clear leaves the old index unreachable.

> **NFR-05, Provenance (passive, and cheap to follow):** The tab's reader of the escapee classification shall have no `transport` or `lib/tauri/*Service` module in its static closure and shall be added to the walk in `exoticProvenanceGraph.test.ts`. No structure that costs a pass over the rows shall be rebuilt when the provenance snapshot changes; only per-species or per-location work may follow a snapshot change. The escapee set is therefore confirmed from the tab's index and not by the shipped per-row confirmation, which serves as the test oracle under FR-35's equality test.

> **NFR-06, Security (user-file text at the surface):** Location ids, location names and protocol names are user-file text. None shall appear in a DOM id, an ARIA IDREF or a CSS selector; a Location ID reaches a URL only through `HotspotLink`'s shape gate and a Submission ID only through `ChecklistLink`'s; every lookup table keyed on such text is a `Map` or is read through `Object.hasOwn`.

> **NFR-07, Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA at 320px wide and at 200% text scale, in both themes. The picker adds exactly two tab stops at any location count; the live region of FR-25 is in the accessibility tree before its first message and holds only the sentence; ranked rows and every other app-owned control render through the `Button` and `Link` primitives; the life list is a list whose every cell is labeled in words for assistive technology, with a visual-only column header row at wide widths (FR-37); the jump row of FR-31 is a named navigation landmark whose chips move focus to the section heading; any chart drawn for FR-48 or FR-52 is decorative beside a text equivalent or is itself readable as text; kind and every state are carried in text, not color alone; any hover look sits inside `@media (hover: hover)`.

> **NFR-08, UI conventions:** Every color through `var(--sr-*)` tokens defined in both themes; responsive layout through classes, never inline layout styles; no horizontal page scroll at 320px and 200% with the longest name the bounds allow; every bird name through `<BirdName>`; the map through `<SnowMap>`; phone-tier rules inside the established 640px tier; any text input carrying a sub-16px size takes `.sr-input-16`.

> **NFR-09, Bundle:** The tab's component, derivation and copy modules stay off the entry chunk, and maplibre and the county geometry stay off it as today; `entryChunk.test.ts` gains rows for the new tab's files as it has for Life Lists. A lazy chart, if one is used, keeps its geometry in a `lib/` module per the house rule.

> **NFR-10, Compatibility:** The derivation is pure over the parsed rows, so the same files give the same report on web/Pi and in every native app. The tab renders in WKWebView and on the Android floor (Chromium 111) with no platform-specific path; any platform difference reads a named predicate in `lib/platformGates.ts`.

> **NFR-11, Copy:** Every user-facing string lives in the tab's copy module; no U+2014 and American spelling throughout; count-bearing sentences are generated and checked by rule over a generated corpus (no count of one with a plural noun or verb); a share for a nonzero count never renders a bare "0%".

> **NFR-12, Verification:** Verification is automated tests plus a live look at the built app on the developer Mac before the deploy gate. No requirement here needs a physical device, and no agent touches one. Rows in Success Metrics marked "live look" cannot be measured in jsdom and are read in the built app in Chromium and WebKit.

## Out of Scope

From the strategic brief, unchanged:

- Reports for hotspots the user has never birded, and eBird's all-observer species list or totals for a hotspot.
- Fetching weather for checklists that carry no block, and any new background fetching.
- Comparing two locations, multi-select, or merging nearby or duplicate locations into one report.
- Exporting, copying, printing or sharing a report.
- Editing, renaming or moving locations.
- Remembering the chosen location across launches, and syncing it.
- Opening a report from a location name elsewhere in the app, and locations in Search.
- Filters inside a report (date range, species, Include Exotics and Include Escapees switches), an embedded media gallery, tide statistics, breeding-code or named-bird sections, and a map of all locations on the general page.
- Changes to Statistics, Map Explorer, Life Lists, Targets or the escapee provenance pass.

Added while writing this PRD:

- **Figures left as they are.** Two older figures for a place use other rules and are not changed; the tab explains them (FR-39, FR-59) instead. (1) Statistics' Geographic Stats top locations by species (`computeGeo`) counts distinct species among the observations Statistics is counting: it never applies the escapee rule, it includes spuhs, slashes and hybrids while Count all forms is on, and it needs no valid date. At Statistics' default its checklist figure also leaves out a checklist whose every row is a non-countable form, which this tab counts. (2) The Map Explorer's My species (`buildHotspotPersonalStats`) applies the countable-form rule, never the escapee rule, needs no valid date, and is shown only for hotspots in a search. (3) The Map Explorer popup's raw "species recorded" line is a fourth figure that Help already explains. So for one place this tab's species total is the lowest of the three whenever the place holds an escapee-only species or a species recorded only on rows with no valid date or no Submission ID, and equal otherwise at Statistics' default.
- The completion status of Statistics' exotic status check on this tab (the Life Lists status line). The safe direction already holds: an unsettled species is listed.
- A Retry control for the hotspot classification, and any change to the hotspot Set, its store or its schedule.
- Sorting controls on the life list, and media marks on its rows.
- Per-band species or duration averages, day and night split, cloud cover, humidity and the other weather-block fields beyond sky condition, temperature and wind.
- A share button, a fullscreen button, a location button or a popup on the report's map (decided at the design review; FR-40, and FR-59 for the Help sentence it re-scopes).
- Matching a location by its Location ID in the picker.
- Capping or windowing the Life Lists Region picker (its own open ROADMAP item); this tab's picker bound is its own.
