Default Location

Set a home location for the Map Explorer. These coordinates load automatically every time you open the map tab.

Alerts

Tells you when eBird reports a species you have never recorded near a place you choose. Only on iPhone and iPad.

Off until you turn it on. Nothing is checked, sent or asked for before then.

Cadence

Hourly checks about once an hour, Daily about once a day. iOS decides when a background check runs. Opening the app also checks, once the interval has passed.

Quiet hours

No alert arrives inside the window. Anything found then goes into the inbox right away and is delivered when the window ends.

Quiet from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM, across midnight. A find at 11:30 PM is delivered at 7:00 AM.

Measure from

Distances are measured from the place below.

Your most recent known position, under the same While Using permission as Use my location. With the app closed, a check uses the last position the app or a widget recorded in the past 24 hours; SnowRaven never asks for Always. When no recent position is known, the fixed place below is used instead.

    iOS asks here, once The system's own notification prompt for SnowRaven appears at this moment, and only the first time you turn alerts on. If you decline, alerts stay on and the inbox still fills; no banner is sent.

    Clear the inbox?

    Removes every alert from this device. Your settings stay as they are, and alerts stay on. A species alerted before may alert again at the next check.

    The status line is one sentence in the widgets' register, chosen in the toolbar above. Six outcomes, not five: a request that never got an answer reads offline; an answer that came back unusable (a server error, a malformed reply) reads eBird did not answer, so "check your connection" is never the wrong advice.
