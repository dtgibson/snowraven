Before (all three surfaces today)

Life Lists

List Comparer

Two life lists side by side, or any two public checklists: what they share and what only one has. (unchanged, for context)

2. What's New on the App Store and the F-Droid changelog line (1.0.70)

After (new; there is no before, each version has its own line)
Life Lists has a Region control: choose a country, state or province, or county from your eBird backup to see that region's life list, each species dated to your first sighting there, and SnowRaven remembers your choice.
