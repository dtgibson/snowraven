SnowRaven · Improve · map-scroll-on-phones · design refinement

On a phone, a map in the page is a still. You use it in fullscreen.

A swipe on the page always moves the page. The map in the page is the real map, kept as you left it and not interactive; pressing it, or Open the map, opens it in fullscreen, where it pans and zooms and every button is whole. The bottom bar steps out of the way while a map is open, so Exit and the share pin are never under it. This is the same form the Family Tree takes for its tree.
