  • Placement. The two dropdowns sit in the media-filter cluster (after the Has-photo/audio pills), since they refine what's in the media. Easy to move beside County if you'd rather group the dropdowns.
  • Active state. A selected facet turns the control accent-green (border + fill + text), exactly like the County filter when a county is chosen.
  • Counts react. Each species' photo/audio/video count shows the filtered subset, and the header "X of N species" updates (it's already an aria-live region).
  • Zero hidden. While a facet is active, species with no matching media drop out of the list (your call, confirmed).
  • Exact-combo. Age + Sex together targets one kind of bird — try Age: Juvenile + Sex: Female and watch the "Adult ♀ / Juv ♂" species fall away.
