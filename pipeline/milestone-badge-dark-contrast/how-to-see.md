## Seeing the dark-mode milestone fix locally

1. The dev servers are running (started for you):
   - App: http://localhost:5173
2. In the app, switch to dark mode (the theme toggle, top-right).
3. Open the Statistics tab and scroll to "Firsts & Milestones".
4. You should see dark green / amber badge tiles with the threshold number, bird name, date,
   and ✓ all clearly readable — no more white tiles or invisible names.
5. Scroll to the bottom (Frivolous Lists) and confirm the "Complete!" badges match.
6. Flip back to light mode and confirm the badges look exactly as before.

(If your eBird backup isn't loaded in this web build, add it in Settings first — the
Milestones section only appears once you've crossed a species threshold.)
