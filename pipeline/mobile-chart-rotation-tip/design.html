How it behaves

  • Phones only. Renders in the ≤640px tier, the app's established phone boundary. Tablets and desktops never see it.
  • Once per page. Statistics and Species Detail each show it once, above their first chart. One shared component, one placement rule.
  • Dismissed means gone. The X collapses it (220ms ease-out; instant under reduced motion) and writes a per-page flag through the storage seam, so it survives relaunches on every platform and never returns.
  • A note, not a nag. Quiet surface, muted text, no accent color, no buttons that steer. Both options are named; the reader decides. Charts are untouched.
  • Accessible. role="note" with a label, a named 32px dismiss control with a visible focus ring, AA contrast in both themes, text scales with the in-app Text Size setting.
