Long lists, and the shapes that aren't lists

Five rows show by default. A bird with more gets the same expander the app already uses on Species Detail's Top Locations, scaled to the card. A bird recorded in only one place gets a sentence instead of a ranking of one, and a bird whose export carries no location names shows no block at all — the same way its map is absent when it has no coordinates.
