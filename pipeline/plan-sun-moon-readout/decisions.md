# Decisions: Plan readout, sun track and moon phase

Stage 4, The Designer (2026-09-12). The design is within `pipeline/design-system.md`; the three items below are deliberate extensions of house patterns, logged so they are not read as drift.

**D4-01. The plan scroller shows its focus ring on plain `:focus` while a pick exists.** The design system's ring is `:focus-visible` only, app-wide. The plan timeline is a slider whose value a POINTER sets (FR-24 says a pointer pick focuses it), and after such a pick the ring is the only cue that the arrow keys now step the pick rather than scroll the page. So `.sr-plan-scroller.has-pick:focus` takes the same inset 3px ring as `:focus-visible`. With no pick nothing changes: a drag leaves no ring, exactly as 1.0.29 shipped. Scoped to this one control; not a change to the global rule.

**D4-02. The fixed readout carries a second line at rest.** The house Fixed-readout pattern (Named Birds) keeps line 2 empty at rest. Here the block is sized for a four-row picked state, so at rest it would be mostly blank space; the second line is the keyboard map (Arrow keys step 15 minutes, Shift an hour, Page Up and Page Down a day, Home and End the ends, Escape clears), which is exactly the "how to pick" FR-17 asks the rest state to state. Muted, 0.71875rem, never announced (the block is `aria-hidden`; the slider's value text at rest is line 1 alone).

**D4-03. The readout cross-fades between rest and picked (140ms, ease-out), and is instant from pick to pick.** The Named Birds readout is instant in every direction, and NFR-10 says the readout has no animation. The figures here never animate: a keyboard step or a new tap replaces them instantly, so a held key scrubs without lag. Only the swap between the instruction lines and the figures cross-fades, and only when reduced motion is off. If the Tester reads NFR-10 as forbidding even that, the swap becomes instant by deleting one transition; nothing else depends on it.

**D4-04. The estimate line stands under the swapped layers and shows at rest too.** FR-13 wants the statement on every pick; FR-17 wants one height for rest and every pick. Putting the line inside the picked layer satisfied both and cost four blank lines at rest at 320px and 200% text scale. As a standing line under the layers it is still present on every pick (QA-13 holds), it is true at rest ("what a pick gives you is an estimate"), and the block at rest shows three useful lines instead of two lines over a hole. The slider's `aria-describedby` still carries it once for assistive technology.

**D4-05 (revision 1). The four band tokens change from their shipped 1.0.29 values, at the user's request.** The first mockup used the shipped `--sr-plan-day` / `--sr-plan-night` exactly (`#FFFFFF` / `#8790A5` light, `#56607A` / `#05070D` dark), and the user's reaction ("is there a reason the whole chart is dark blue grey?") is the honest reading of them over a September window: most of the plot is a blue-grey slab in light, and in dark the lit day band is itself blue-grey. New values: light `#FFFCF5` day / `#CBD1DE` night; dark `#3E3830` day / `#121317` night, with the dark halo following the night ground to `#121317`. The one guard row that changes is `planContrast.test.ts`'s "night clears 3:1 against day", which becomes a 1.4:1 visible-boundary floor: both bands are always drawn and in the legend, every boundary is also marked by a sunrise or sunset triangle with its label, and the sun line now crosses the horizon there too, so the band is reinforcement and not the only carrier. Every other row passes with more margin than before (the full table is in the spec). The design system's Planner-chart paragraph is updated with the values and the floor. `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` and `ACCESSIBILITY.md` are unaffected.

**D4-06 (revision 1). The sun line is warm and filled only above the horizon; below it, a 1px receded line in a new token, `--sr-plan-sunline-night`.** Drawn rather than dropped, because the readout's "Sun 8° below the horizon" should meet a drawn line at the marker and a keyboard user stepping through the night keeps a continuous curve; a token rather than an alpha of the ink, so FR-31's "every color of the track clears 3:1 against both bands" stays literally true and the guard gains one plain row. If the user prefers the night portion gone, it is one polyline omitted and the legend's tails removed.

**D4-07 (revision 1). The shipped wide-tier two-column day list becomes one chronological column at every tier.** The two CSS columns are 1.0.29's shipped layout (`@container plan (min-width: 760px) { .sr-plan-days { columns: 2 } }`), and the user found them confusing: reading across a row puts a later day beside an earlier one. The list is now a single column, earliest first, capped at 44rem, below the chart; the chart keeps the full card width as the focal point. This supersedes the 1.0.29 spec's two-column paragraph, and the Engineer changes the stylesheet the real `PlanResult` renders with, not only the mockup.

**D4-08 (revision 1). The partial first day says so, and never names a past moment.** Its heading carries "· from {fetch time}" (true on a replayed plan, whose cue says when it was fetched), and when solar noon is already behind the fetch instant its sun clause reads "Sun past its highest today, {n}° above the horizon at {fetch time}" instead of a peak at a time that has passed. Every other entry is a whole day ahead.

**D4-09 (revision 2). A labelled rule divides the timeline from the week, and its label becomes the list's accessible name.** The user asked for a divider between "the chart's display area (from moving the pin around)" and the week of daily entries. It is a section-label-register rule ("DAY BY DAY" with the plan action's calendar glyph, 22px above, a 1px `--sr-border-medium` line to the right edge), chosen over a change of ground for the list because the region is already a tinted panel inside a card and a third ground would nest. The visible words name the list (`aria-labelledby`) in place of 1.0.29's `aria-label` "Sunrises and sunsets ahead", which no longer described a list carrying tides, sky, moon and sun; one string changes, no heading level is added, and the guard that pairs visible labels with accessible names has one fewer mismatch to reason about.

**Mockup defect, revision 2 (not a design decision, recorded so the record is honest).** Rounds 0 and 1 of the mockup drew every night after the first as a zero-width rect and listed "Sunset 7:19 PM" on every day, because the mockup's fixture dropped the day offset from each day's listed sunrise and sunset (every event landed on day 0). The user's "the whole chart is dark blue grey" in round 1 was therefore partly this bug: one night band drawn across the whole week. Fixed in the fixture; the verification now asserts eight positive, strictly increasing night rects summing to less than the plot, alternating day and night track runs of positive extent, eight fills, fifteen event triangles and a stable divider. The band repaint (D4-05) stands on its own merits and was re-checked against the corrected render.

**Numbers that move (geometry, not the design system).** `PLAN_PLOT_NO_TIDE_PX` 40 → 72 and `PLAN_WIDE_PLOT_NO_TIDE_PX` 56 → 120, so the without-tide plot holds the sun track; every other lane height is unchanged. `PLAN_SUN_ZERO_FRAC = 0.6` from the top, domain [−60, 90]. Reasons in `design-spec.md`, "Numbers the Engineer needs".

**Tokens added (additive, both themes).** `--sr-plan-sunline` (light `#703000`, dark `#FDBA74`), `--sr-plan-sunline-rgb`, `--sr-plan-pick` (light `#0F3D22`, dark `#34D399`). Each clears 3:1 against both bands in both themes; ratios in the spec. Hue reasoning: in the light theme anything that clears the slate night band AND white must be dark, so the sun line is a deep burnt orange in the existing sunrise/sunset family and the marker is the accent's deepest green (the house "active" color at the depth the bands require).

**D4-10 (design gate, 2026-09-12). The user approved the direction as rendered after revision 2 and settled the two open choices:** the second action keeps the user's own words, "See all upcoming weather and tide data" (the proposed tightening is declined and not built), and the sun line's receded night portion (D4-06) stays as drawn.
