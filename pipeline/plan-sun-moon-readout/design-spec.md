# Design Spec: Plan readout, sun track and moon phase

**Feature:** plan-sun-moon-readout
**Stage:** 4, The Designer (first direction 2026-09-12; revision 1 the same day, from the user's look: the bands repainted, the sun line receded below the horizon, the per-day list one chronological column)
**Mockup:** `pipeline/plan-sun-moon-readout/design.html` (self-contained; theme, width (Desktop 1440 / Tablet 768 / Phone 390 / Phone 320), text size, motion, second-action label and state switchers in the top bar, plus a Pick menu that lands a pick exactly as a tap would (it focuses the timeline and never scrolls the box; use the arrow keys on the focused timeline to see the reveal scroll); deep links `?theme=dark&w=phone-320&text=2&state=toofar&pick=sun-0607&rm=1&label=proposed&days=3`)
**Design system:** `pipeline/design-system.md` applies in full. This is an addition to the 1.0.29 Planner's approved design (`pipeline/tide-weather-planner/design-spec.md`), which stands wherever this document is silent, with two deliberate changes to what shipped, both at the user's request in revision 1 and both logged in `decisions.md`: the four band tokens take new values, and the wide tier's two-column day list becomes one chronological column. Four additive tokens; the house-pattern extensions are logged there too.

## Visual Direction
The Planner keeps its one focal point, the chart, and the chart gains a second line under the tide: the sun's height across the window, drawn low and warm beneath everything else so it reads as the light the day will have rather than as a second data series competing with the tide. The plot itself now reads as daylight and night rather than as one blue-grey field: in the light theme the day band is barely warmed paper and the night band a quiet cool tone a step darker; in the dark theme the day band is a warm lit panel above the surface and the night band recedes to just under it. The sun line is warm, and filled beneath, only while the sun is up; below the horizon it thins to a receded slate line with no fill, so the warm color itself means daylight. Picking a moment puts one solid marker on the chart and fills one quiet block beneath the legend with that moment's figures, in the list's own words and registers, so nothing new has to be learned to read it. The per-day list gains one line per day naming the moon and the sun's high point. The entry now reads Plan. Quiet utility throughout: the green stays on the actions, the focus ring and the picked marker; nothing is ranked, scored or highlighted; every moment and every day gets identical treatment.

## Screens / Views

### The entry (Weather tab, Plan button and the opened form)
- The mode row is unchanged in shape: **Current** (filled) beside **Plan** (outline, the same calendar-clock glyph). Plan's accessible name: **Plan weather and tide for a place**. Current's label and name are byte-identical to 1.0.29.
- The opened form is the shipped form: place search, map pin, latitude and longitude, date and time. At its foot, two full-width actions, one above the other, in the same weights as 1.0.29 (OQ-03, unchanged):
  - **Get specific forecast** (primary, filled `--sr-accent`, the sun-on-horizon glyph). It sits directly under the date and time fields it reads.
  - **See all upcoming weather and tide data** (secondary, the outline register: `--sr-accent-bg` fill, 1.5px `--sr-accent-border`, `--sr-accent` text, `CalendarDays` glyph, 8px beneath).
  - **Both labels wrap on the phone tier.** The renamed primary is nine characters longer than "Get forecast" and clips inside a `white-space: nowrap` 44px button at 320px and 200% text scale (measured in the mockup before the fix), so on the ≤640 tier both action buttons take `white-space: normal; height: auto; min-height: 44px; padding-block: 8px; line-height: 1.3`, the way the shipped `.sr-btn-*` registers already do at that tier. At 390px the second label wraps once either way; nothing else in the form changes.
  - The caption beneath, unchanged apart from the rename: **The plan runs from now to the end of the forecast, about eight days, for the place above. The date and time apply only to Get specific forecast.**
- **OQ-04 is resolved: the second label ships as the user's words, "See all upcoming weather and tide data".** The tightening the mockup offered under its "2nd action: Proposed" switch ("See weather and tide for the days ahead") was declined by the user at the design gate and is not built. Both labels wrap once at 390px; the Engineer allows the wrap (the shipped nowrap 44px button clips "Get specific forecast" at 320px/200%).

### The plan timeline with the sun track
Everything 1.0.29 draws is drawn as before: the band spans, the curve, the turning points, the sunrise and sunset triangles with their leaders and labels, the Now hairline, the axis lane, the strip, the gutter, the day-header lane and Days in view on the wide tier. Two things change and two are added.

- **The bands are repainted (revision 1).** The mockup's first round used the shipped 1.0.29 tokens, `#FFFFFF` day over `#8790A5` night in light and `#56607A` day over `#05070D` night in dark, and with September nights on this axis the plot read as one blue-grey slab. Now, light: the day band is `#FFFCF5`, a barely warmed daylight tint on the white chart surface, and the night band is `#CBD1DE`, a quiet cool grey a step darker (1.49:1 apart). Dark: the day band is `#3E3830`, a warm lit umber 1.53:1 above the `--sr-surface` ground, and the night band is `#121317`, which recedes to just under the surface (1.05:1) instead of being a second dark slab; the halo follows the night ground as before. The day/night boundary is carried by the sunrise and sunset marks, their labels and the sun line's own crossing; the band is reinforcement, so the shipped "night clears 3:1 against day" guard row becomes "the bands differ by at least 1.4:1" (the visible-boundary floor the design system already accepts for the sticky band), and every mark, line and label row of that guard passes with more margin than before (ratios under Design Tokens Applied). Both bands stay in the legend.
- **The sun track sits inside the plot on its own scale (OQ-08, the default taken).** Its zero line is at **60% of the plot's height from the top** (`PLAN_SUN_ZERO_FRAC = 0.6`), so daylight occupies the upper 60% and night the lower 40% on one uniform scale: 90° at the plot's top, 0° at 60%, −60° at the bottom (one degree is plot / 150). A Monterey day peaks around 57°, a quarter of the way down the plot; a Monterey night bottoms near −50°, just above the plot's foot. No sun scale is drawn and no zero line is drawn: the track crosses zero exactly at the night bands' edges by construction, which is the reference a reader sees, and the readout states the degrees. The legend names it.
- **Style, split at the horizon (revision 1).** While the sun is UP: a 1.5px `--sr-plan-sunline` line with round joins through the model's samples (one per 15 minutes plus the anchors, never resampled with density), and beneath it a fill from the zero line up to the arc in `rgba(var(--sr-plan-sunline-rgb), 0.14)`, so each day reads as a low warm hill. While the sun is DOWN: a 1px line in `--sr-plan-sunline-night` (a receded slate in light, a dim grey in dark), no fill, no dash (dashes mean Now and leaders on this chart). The crossing samples belong to both runs, so the two meet at the horizon and the curve stays continuous. Chosen over not drawing the night portion at all: the readout says "Sun 8° below the horizon" and a keyboard user stepping through the night should meet a drawn line at the marker, and the depth of the night (how far below) stays readable; what disappears is the warm color and the fill, which now exist only in daylight, which is what the user asked the line to mean. Order: night lines, then the daytime fills, then the daytime lines, all after the bands and gridlines and BEFORE the Now hairline, the tide curve, the turning points and the event markers (SVG order, QA-30). The fill is the non-color cue against the tide (a 2px unfilled line).
- **Without tide** the track still draws, the sunrise and sunset markers stay on the axis line as shipped (the leader from the label lane runs down to them; the track crosses zero at the same x, higher up), and the plot grows to hold the hill: **`PLAN_PLOT_NO_TIDE_PX` 40 → 72** (phone) and **`PLAN_WIDE_PLOT_NO_TIDE_PX` 56 → 120** (wide). Chart heights without tide become 186 (phone) and 240 (wide); with tide they stay 242 and 340. The gutter's tide scale is untouched and hidden without tide, as shipped.
- **Polar and one-event days.** A day listing neither event draws the unanchored computed curve (the Utqiagvik state: a warm filled hill that never touches zero, no band); a polar night would be the receded line alone; a day listing one event crosses zero once, at it, and is held on the listed side elsewhere. The list's "No sunrise this day." / "No sunset this day." notes are unchanged.
- **The pick marker** (an HTML overlay inside the canvas, positioned by the same `x(t)`, never a chart-library line): a 2px solid `--sr-plan-pick` line the plot's full height with a 1px `--sr-plan-halo` on each side, and a 12 × 7 px pill handle in the same color with a 1.5px halo ring sitting on the plot's top edge. Solid and handled against the Now hairline's dashed 1px; a line against every circle and triangle. It moves without animation, ever. It scrolls with the track, is re-placed on a Days in view change, a resize or a tier change, survives the override, and is removed by Escape, a new plan or anything that replaces the result. Identical for every instant (FR-44).
- **The legend** gains, after Low (or after Sunset when tide is absent): a 24 × 12 swatch showing the warm filled hill with a receded 1px tail on each side (the night portion), and the words **Sun height**. Nothing is added for the marker: the readout beneath carries the same glyph and explains it.
- **Focus.** The scroller keeps the inset 3px `--sr-accent` ring on `:focus-visible`, and additionally shows it on plain `:focus` while a pick exists (`.has-pick:focus`): a pointer pick focuses the slider (FR-24), and the ring is how a sighted user learns the arrow keys now step the pick. With no pick, a drag leaves no ring, exactly as 1.0.29. Logged in `decisions.md`.
- **Cursor** stays `grab` / `grabbing`: the surface is still primarily dragged; a press that does not move picks.
- **Hover previews nothing (OQ-02, none).** Reasons: the pointer is always over the chart on its way to the day buttons, so a hover readout would churn through every minute it crosses; touch has no hover, so the phone and desktop would behave differently; and a committed pick already has a marker and a readout, so an uncommitted second state adds a thing to explain and nothing to read.

### The picked-moment readout (rest and picked)
One block in normal flow between the legend row and the per-day list, `aria-hidden`, rem sizes, rendered from first paint with the list and never unmounted. A 1px `--sr-border` top rule, 11px of padding above, 12px above the rule. It has two parts: the SWAPPED LAYERS (rest text or picked figures, one grid cell) and, beneath them in both states, the ESTIMATE LINE. The layers' content is a 14px glyph column beside a text column (grid `14px minmax(0, 1fr)`, 9px gap). The glyph is the pick marker in miniature (a line with its pill handle, inline SVG, `currentColor`): `--sr-plan-pick` when a moment is picked, `--sr-border-medium` at rest, so the empty marker itself says "nothing picked".

**At rest** (two lines, both `--sr-text-muted`):
1. 0.8125rem: **Tap or click the timeline, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to read the tide, weather and sun height at any moment.**
2. 0.71875rem: **Arrow keys step 15 minutes, with Shift an hour. Page Up and Page Down move a day, Home and End go to the ends, Escape clears.**

**Beneath the layers, in both states** (0.71875rem muted, indented to the text column): **Estimated from the plan, not a fresh lookup. For an exact moment, use Get specific forecast.** It is true at rest (it says what a pick will give) and it is the FR-13 statement on every pick; standing outside the swapped layers, it adds nothing to the space the picked figures reserve, which at 320px and 200% text scale is four fewer lines of blank at rest.

**Picked** (three rows in the list's own registers, over the standing estimate line):
1. **When**, the display row: the day in 0.8125rem/600 ("Sun, Sep 13") then the time in 1.0625rem/700 tabular ("6:07 AM"), baseline-aligned, wrapping at 200%. The same pair the event items use, so a pick reads like an event.
2. **The three figures**, 0.8125rem, one wrapping run separated by muted middots: **Tide 2.9 ft, rising** · 🌫️ **Fog, 55°F** (a daily reading adds the muted "(H 71° · L 56°)") · **Sun 8° below the horizon**. Figures in 600; the no-tide, no-weather and trend-unknown phrases in plain weight.
3. **The rest of the weather**, 0.75rem muted with the values in ink: **Wind Mostly calm, SW · Humidity 93% · Dew pt 54°F · Cloud 100%**, ending with the resolution micro-label **FORECAST · HOURLY** / **FORECAST · DAILY** (0.625rem/700 uppercase, on its own line on phones). Omitted entirely when the plan holds no cell for the instant (the sizer keeps the height). All six weather fields are carried (OQ-05, the default): a birder reading a fogbound shoreline wants the humidity and dew point beside the condition, and the list already prints exactly these six in this order, so the readout adds no vocabulary.

**Height rule (schema 6.2, D13).** Three layers share one grid cell (`grid-area: 1 / 1`): a SIZER that is always `visibility: hidden` and renders the longest strings this document can produce; the rest text; the picked figures. The cell is as tall as the tallest layer at the current width and text scale, so rest, a full pick, a no-tide pick, a no-weather pick and a daily-tail pick all render at one height and nothing below or above moves (QA-17). The sizer's strings, per document: the longest formatted day label in the window; "12:00 PM"; the tide phrase **Tide −0.0 ft, trend unknown (no later high or low in the data)** ONLY when this document can produce it (its last untrimmed turning point lies before the window's end), else **Tide −0.0 ft, falling** (a no-tide plan is still sized for a tide figure, so the override changes nothing about the height); the longest `description` among the document's cells with **100°F**, plus **(H 100° · L 100°)** when the document holds a daily cell; **Sun 90° below the horizon**; the longest `windDesc` among the cells with **NW**, then **100% · 100°F · 100%** and the hourly label. The no-tide and no-weather phrases are shorter than what they replace and never size the block. Measured in the mockup (Chrome), identical at rest, on an hourly pick, on a daily-tail pick, at the window's end and on a no-tide pick: **773px** for the whole block at 320px and 200% text scale (585px of it the swapped layers), **195px** (145px) at 390px and 100%, **103px** (70px) at 1440px; the list's top edge does not move in any case, and `scrollWidth === clientWidth` at every tier with a pick on screen. The cost this buys: at rest the block reserves the picked height, which at a 390px phone leaves about 90px under the two instruction lines and at 1440px about 35px; that is the price FR-17 sets, and the standing estimate line, the sizer trimmed to the document and the keyboard line at rest are the three things that keep it small.

**Cross-fade.** Rest and picked are two layers, so the change between them is a 140ms ease-out opacity cross-fade (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`), visibility switching at its end. A change from one pick to another (every keyboard step, every new tap) replaces the figures instantly, the way the Named Birds readout does, so a held arrow key reads as a scrub and the block never lags the marker. Under reduced motion the cross-fade is removed. Logged in `decisions.md` against NFR-10's "the readout has no animation": the figures never animate; only the swap between the instruction and the figures does, and not under reduced motion.

### The divider between the timeline and the week (revision 2)
The region has two parts: the interactive timeline and what it reads out (the Days in view control, the chart box, the legend, the fixed readout), and the week day by day beneath. Between them, a labelled rule in the section-label register: 22px of air below the readout block, then a 13px `CalendarDays` glyph (the plan action's own icon, muted) and the words **DAY BY DAY** (0.6875rem/700, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, `--sr-text-muted`), then a 1px `--sr-border-medium` rule running to the region's right edge, a step stronger than the dashed `--sr-border` rules between days so it reads as a section boundary rather than one more day break; 12px below it the first day begins. The label is the list's accessible name (`<ol aria-labelledby>` → the label's `useId()`-based id; it replaces the 1.0.29 `aria-label` "Sunrises and sunsets ahead", which no longer describes a list that also carries tides, sky, moon and sun; logged as D4-09). It is not a heading, so the h3 place → h4 day hierarchy is untouched. Same tokens in both themes; at 320px and 200% the label stays on one line and the rule takes what is left (minimum 24px). Because the readout above it is a fixed-height block, the divider's position is identical at rest and on any pick (asserted in the mockup's verification).

### The per-day list (one chronological column; moon line, sun peak line)
**One column, earliest day first, at every tier (revision 1).** The shipped 1.0.29 wide tier flows the list into two CSS columns above 760px of region width (`@container plan (min-width: 760px) { .sr-plan-days { columns: 2 } }` in `globals.css`, days 1 to 4 down the left and 5 to 8 down the right), which the eye reads across rows, putting later days beside earlier ones. This run deliberately changes that shipped layout, at the user's request: the container rule and `break-inside` go, `.sr-plan-days` takes `max-width: 44rem` (a reading measure that scales with the in-app text size, so at 200% it is effectively the region's width), and the list sits BELOW the chart, left-aligned to the region's content edge under the readout, at every tier. The chart keeps the full 1080px card as the wide focal point; the whitespace to the right of the column is deliberate. Nothing about the items changes. The Engineer changes the real component's stylesheet, not only the mockup, and the 1.0.29 spec's "Wide plan regions flow the list into two columns" paragraph is superseded by this one.

**Nothing in the list reads as a past day.** The list already omits every event before Now and every reading before the axis start. Two things make the partial first day explicit: its heading gains the fetch instant, **SAT, SEP 12 · FROM 3:41 PM** (`.from`, weight 600, the same micro-caps register), so the entry is clearly "the rest of today" and stays true on a replayed plan (the cue above says when it was fetched); and its sun clause never names a past moment (below).

Each day's entry gains ONE line directly under its heading, before the tides line, in the tides line's register (0.75rem, `--sr-text-muted`, values in ink 600, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`): **🌒 Waxing crescent · Sun highest at 1:03 PM, 57° above the horizon**. The glyph is `aria-hidden`; the name is text. On a day whose peak is below zero (a polar night) the same template reads **Sun highest at 12:41 PM, 3° below the horizon**. **On the first day, when solar noon is already behind the fetch instant** (a plan made in the afternoon), the clause is instead **Sun past its highest today, 42° above the horizon at 3:41 PM** (the anchored altitude at Now; the same wording with "below" after sunset), so the entry states an upcoming fact rather than the peak at a time that has passed. A day the sun model could not sample carries the moon alone. Order within a day's entry: heading → moon and sun peak → tides → sky → notes → events. The events, the tides line and the sky line are byte-unchanged.

**The moon stays off the timeline (OQ-06, the default).** The phase is a fact about the day, not a moment on the axis; the label lane and the day-header lane are at capacity at All on the wide tier; and a glyph per day would need a density rule of its own at 3 days per 200px. If it is wanted later, the day-header lane's right end is where it goes.

## Component Usage
- `PlanResult.tsx` (static): the readout block (three layers), the pick state, the day-facts line, the Sun height legend entry, the memoised list. `PlanChart.tsx` (lazy): the track, the marker overlay, the slider role and attributes, the key map, the pointer machine, the reveal scroll.
- The scroller keeps the same `div` with the same `tabIndex={0}` literal; only its role changes (`img` → `slider`). No new `Button`; the day buttons and Days in view are unchanged.
- Lucide: none new. The readout glyph is the marker's own shape as inline SVG, not an icon.
- The moon glyph is the string `moonPhaseEmoji` returns (never a lucide moon); the name is `MOON_PHASE_NAMES[phase]`.
- No new library, no shadcn component; the Weather tab stays hand-styled.

## Interaction Notes
- **The slider (schema 6.3, D10).** `.sr-plan-scroller` carries `role="slider"`, `aria-orientation="horizontal"`, `aria-valuemin` = the axis start, `aria-valuemax` = the window's last whole minute, `aria-valuenow` = the pick or Now, `aria-valuetext` = the four figures on a pick or the rest line at rest, `aria-label` = the timeline name below, `aria-describedby` → a visually hidden span holding the estimate line once. Everything inside stays `aria-hidden` + `inert`. No live region anywhere in the region. Blur does not clear (D11).
- **The readout follows the committed pick only**; it is `aria-hidden` because the slider's value text says the same words.
- **Pointer (OQ-09 at 5 px).** Mouse: a press and release that moved under 5 CSS px in total picks the instant under the PRESS point and focuses the slider (`preventScroll`); anything more is the shipped one-to-one drag under pointer capture and neither picks nor clears. Touch and pen: native scrolling; a tap picks; a cancelled pointer never picks. A press in the gutter picks the axis start. A pointer pick never scrolls the box.
- **Keys** (the timeline focused): Left / Right step to the previous / next 15-minute mark of the location's clock; Shift+Left / Shift+Right an hour; Page Down a day earlier and Page Up a day later (24 hours of real time); Home the axis start; End the window's end; Escape clears (consumed only while a pick exists); Enter, Space, Up and Down unbound. With no pick, the first step is taken from Now (3:41 PM → Right 3:45 PM, Left 3:30 PM); from an unaligned pointer pick the first step aligns (6:07 → Right 6:15, Left 6:00); hour and day steps align first, then move. Every step clamps at the ends.
- **What the box follows.** A keyboard step whose marker leaves the visible span scrolls the box by the minimum that shows the marker (with the gutter's 40px kept clear on the left), `smooth` unless reduced motion; issued from the key handler, never from an effect, so no pointer path ever scrolls and nothing runs after a drag (FR-22).
- **Tab order:** unchanged. Days in view → the timeline (one stop, however many days) → Earlier day → Later day. The readout and the marker are never focusable.
- **Timeline name.** With tide: **Plan timeline for {place}, {start} to {end}. The arrow keys read the tide, weather and sun height at any moment. Details are in the list below.** Without: **Plan timeline for {place}, {start} to {end}; no tide is shown. The arrow keys read the weather and sun height at any moment.
