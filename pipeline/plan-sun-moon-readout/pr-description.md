## Plan readout, sun track and moon phase

### What this does
The Weather tab's Predict entry becomes Plan, its two actions become "Get specific forecast" and "See all upcoming weather and tide data", and the Weather/tide Planner's timeline gains three layers derived from the plan document already on the device: a pointer or keyboard pick at any minute that fills a fixed readout with that moment's local time, tide height and trend, weather with its hourly or daily label, and sun altitude; a sun-altitude track drawn beneath the tide curve, anchored so it crosses zero exactly at the listed sunrise and sunset; and a moon phase line on every day of the list, from the checklist weather blocks' own moon function. No new provider, no new request, no field added to any stored document, so a plan stored by 1.0.29 replays with all three layers. The day list is one chronological column at every width under a labelled Day by day divider, and the chart's day and night bands are repainted per the approved design. Version 1.0.30 (the four-file set) with the changelog entry.

### Notes for reviewer
Decisions taken where the artifacts left room, each with the reason:
- **`lib/planPick.ts` rides the lazy chart chunk, not the entry graph.** The schema's seam table listed it on the entry graph, but only `PlanChart.tsx` steps a pick; nothing static imports it. `entryChunk.test.ts` asserts it OFF the entry graph and reached by the chart closure. The other four modules (`tzClock`, `planSun`, `planMoon`, `planReadout`) join the entry graph as the schema says, each with a dependency-light closure assertion.
- **The sun track is a bare SVG child of the Recharts chart, with the bands at z-index -100 and the gridlines and midnight lines at -50.** Recharts 3.8 renders by z-index layers, not child order, and a `ZIndexLayer` at a value it has no default layer for (250) was measured registering late and emptying the reference items' portals when the layer set changed. A bare child renders between the two negative layers Recharts always draws and layer 100, which puts the track above the bands and gridlines and beneath the Now hairline, the tide curve and every marker; `PlanChart.test.tsx` pins that order by layer.
- **The sun domain is [-60, 90].** The schema wrote `min = -90 * f / (1 - f)` with the fraction from the bottom; the design's fraction is from the TOP (0.6), so `planSunDomain()` is `-90 * (1 - f) / f = -60`, as the design spec's own worked arithmetic states. The first cut had the schema's form and the geometry test caught it.
- **The divider's 24px hairline floor is a flex basis (`flex: 1 1 24px`), not `min-width: 24px`.** `planCss.test.ts` forbids any positive `min-width` in the planner (nothing may widen the region); the row is far wider than the floor at every reachable width, so the rendered result is identical.
