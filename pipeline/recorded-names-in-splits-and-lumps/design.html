Splits/Lumps/Renames: recorded names on each earlier name

How to read this page. Anything inside a dashed frame is what the app would draw, in its own type, color and spacing. Anything outside a frame, in this typewriter face, is a note from the designer. The buttons at the top switch every frame between light and dark, wide and a 320px phone, and normal and doubled text.

Which figures are real. The Whimbrel figures are yours: 25 reports under Hudsonian Whimbrel, 14 dated on or before 31 October 2025 and 11 after, and none under Eurasian Whimbrel. Every other count on this page is illustrative. The species, the updates and their dates are real, from the taxonomy files the app ships from.

Design system. This sits inside SnowRaven's established look: no new color, type or component. The few new rules are listed at the foot of the page.

Lump: four ways it can read

1. Traced eBird kept each earlier name as a form

A report dated on or before the lump that still carries a form is counted under the name that form came from, and the box names the form. Reports with no form go to their own dashed box, "Earlier name not recorded", never to a guessed name. The three boxes on the left add up to the 58 "reassigned by eBird" on the right, and the thin gray bars show how the 58 divide.

In the app: Species Detail, Western Flycatcher

2. Traced, with no reports what your own Western Flycatcher will show

Your backup starts after this lump, so both earlier names are in the zero state: the backup could show such reports and holds none. This must not read like "Not traceable in your backup" two frames down. The wording here is mine and I would like your eye on it.

In the app: Species Detail, Western Flycatcher, every report dated after the lump

3. Traced in part one name kept as a form, one name carried on

Collared Trogon kept its name through the lump, so an older plain Collared Trogon report could have been either species, or the old slash. It reads "Not traceable in your backup" with the reason, and the plain reports sit in "Earlier name not recorded". About half of the covered lumps (52 of 101) have at least one name like this.

In the app: Species Detail, Collared Trogon

4. Not traceable eBird kept no form

American Crow kept its name and Northwestern Crow was given no form, so nothing on the left can be counted and nothing is guessed. Each box gives its own reason. There is no "Earlier name not recorded" box and no explanation paragraph, because no count is shown.

In the app: Species Detail, American Crow

Rename: Thick-billed Longspur, by September 2021

The third kind. A rename is dated only to the yearly update whose file first carries the new name, so nothing here says the name changed at the update: the badge reads "by September 2021", the earlier name's line ends "at the latest", and the paragraph says what that means for the count. The right-hand box reads "renamed by eBird" where a split reads "reassigned by eBird".

None of your own species was renamed in the covered updates, so you will meet this only on a different backup.

In the app: Species Detail, Thick-billed Longspur

A rename whose spelling moved again and a count of one

eBird's 2021 file spells the new name "Nankeen Night-Heron"; today it is "Nankeen Night Heron". The box and the sentence use today's spelling, the one the rest of the app shows for this bird. Also the singular: "1 report", "eBird's name for it".

In the app: Species Detail, Nankeen Night Heron

A later change: Great Horned Owl, 2022 and then 2026

Two splits on one species, each on its own line with its own date, as today. The 2026 split moved some reports to new names, so a count on the 2022 line would miss them. The 2022 line's earlier name therefore carries a marker and no number, and the 2026 line counts as usual. Tropical Horned Owl shows a current name the birder also holds: it keeps its link and its two site marks, as shipped.

In the app: Species Detail, Great Horned Owl

A backup exported before an update: unchanged

Exactly as the app draws it today. The backup still carries Whimbrel, so the reports are counted as Whimbrel reports, directly. None of the new pieces appears: no "until the update" count, no "Earlier name not recorded", no "Not traceable", no explanation paragraph. A report is never counted both ways.

One combination to know about: a backup older than the first of two renames shows the first rename like this frame and the second with "No reports until the update", because none of its reports carries the middle name yet. The zero state's line is worded so it stays true there.

In the app, as shipped: Species Detail, Whimbrel, backup exported before October 2025

Wording that is mine, for your eye

Fixed and not reopened here: the title "Splits/Lumps/Renames", "Earlier name not recorded", "Not traceable in your backup", the word "Rename", and "eBird's name for them until the update". Everything below is a proposal.

  • The control's label
    Splits/Lumps/Renames
    The same string as the title. The other choice is sentence form, "Splits, lumps, and renames", to sit beside "Subspecies and forms".
  • A species with no change
    No split, lump, or rename is recorded for this species in the covered updates.
    Today's line with the third kind added. "Is recorded" on purpose: it is a statement about the record.
  • An earlier name with nothing to count
    No reports until the update
    Followed by one line saying what was looked for: for a lump, "None dated on or before it carries the form Western Flycatcher (Cordilleran)."
  • A rename, three places
    Rename, by September 2021 · eBird's name for them until the update, at the latest · 9 renamed by eBird
    "By" and "at the latest" carry the dating limit without leaving the fixed wording. If you would sooner keep the earlier name's line identical on all three kinds, the paragraph alone can carry it.
  • A traced lump name
    now the form Western Flycatcher (Pacific-slope)
    Names the form the count was worked out from, in the words a birder would see on the report today.
  • Not traceable, the two reasons
    eBird kept no form for this name, so its reports now read as plain American Crow. · This name carried on as the lumped species, so its earlier reports read the same as any report with no form.
    One sentence per box, each true for its own reason.
  • An earlier name on a line a later update changed
    Not counted on this line · A later update changed one of the names it became.
    No number and no zero, so it cannot be read as "none".
  • The list's opening line
    Was "that eBird split or lumped". Worded as what is recorded so it never claims a species was not renamed.

Notes for the build

What is new in the stylesheet. Eight small rules, all marked PROPOSED in this page's source: a break opportunity after each slash in the title and the control's label; a list row that lets its kinds drop under a long name; the stacked chart's badge allowed to wrap its date under the word "Rename"; the second paragraph's spacing; a form name inheriting its line's type; a marker that leads a box; the glyph on a "cannot be counted" marker; and the earlier name's share bar. No new token, no new component, no new control.

Motion. None added. The list's pop-in from its control (200ms, ease-out, from the top left), the caret's turn, the section's 160ms entrance when the species changes and the 240ms bar fill are the shipped ones, and the new share bar joins that same fill. All of it collapses under reduced motion. The stills in the gallery do not animate.

Sizes and thresholds. One threshold moves. The chart stacks when its own event box (.sr-lin-event) is narrow; today that is at most 21em or 420px, and here it is at most 29em or 520px, because "Not traceable in your backup" and the wider "Rename by ..." badge need more room side by side. Measured in this mockup's frames in both engines: three columns fit from an event box 490px wide at 100% text, 550px at 125%, 610px at 150% and 730px at 200%. The effect a user could notice: at 200% text the chart stays stacked until the event box is about 930px wide, where today it goes side by side at about 670px. The phone rules still start at a 640px window. All of it is to be measured again in the built app.

Hover. The control and list hover looks sit inside a hover media query here, so a touch screen does not keep them after a tap. The shipped rules for these two are not gated yet.
