Find a bird by typing,
not by scrolling

The My Sightings filter panel's Species control is the app's last scroll-only species dropdown. It becomes the same type-to-find picker Species Detail and the Calendar already use. What it filters and how results render do not change.

Fit: one new size, panel

SpeciesCombobox gains a third size that IS the panel register: 34px tall, 0.8125rem text, 6px radius, full panel width - the exact SELECT_STYLE numbers every neighbouring control uses. The existing sm (Calendar) and md (Species Detail) code paths are untouched, so both stay regression-free by construction.

The open list never clips

The filter panel's collapse wrapper releases its clip while the panel is open (overflow: hidden only while collapsed or animating). The open list paints over the filters below it, and the sidebar or sheet scrollport keeps every row reachable - at 320px width and 200% text scale. Internal scroll caps the list at its existing 260px.

Today, next to the refinement

Desktop sidebar, closed state. Same 34px register, same label, same width. The only visible change is the search icon, the type-in affordance, and the chevron.
