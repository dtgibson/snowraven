Designer notes for this review (revision 2: subtler)

  • Subtler, per your feedback. The Heart icon tile, the "With thanks" title, and the description line are gone. The section is now the tab's quietest register (the Troubleshooting shape): the uppercase section header names it, and the card holds one plain bordered button. No accent slab, no flourish.
  • Reveal: inline disclosure, not an overlay (unchanged). The panel expands inside the card, directly under the button. Collapse keeps focus on the button; the collapsed panel is inert (unreachable by Tab and hidden from assistive technology).
  • Placement: last section of the Settings tab (unchanged), after Troubleshooting on desktop, after Tab Layout on web and iOS where Troubleshooting does not render. No existing section moves.
  • No outbound links (unchanged); the Deven Simonson entry never links regardless.
  • Entries: approved copy kept verbatim, each as a name line plus a "For …" line.
  • Motion: the panel grows from the toggle row (grid-collapse, 200ms ease-out, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1), the app's shipped listbox easing) with a small settle from above; the button goes from the quiet bordered treatment to the app's standard accent-tinted active state while open. Reduced motion renders both near-instantly.
  • 320px and 200%: use the toolbar toggles above. The button takes the 44px touch-target posture and the panel text wraps with no horizontal scroll. In the real build these hang off the existing ≤640/≤480 tiers.
  • Illustrative context: every section above the new one is existing shipped UI, reproduced here (some rows condensed) so the new section can be judged in place; nothing about them changes.
