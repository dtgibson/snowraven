The replicas use the app's own row markup, tokens, sizes and truncation. Nothing on a row says why it is there; the dashed notes are for this review only. Arrow keys, Enter and clicks work in each replica; the real Search closes after Enter, and the real picker closes after a pick.

The list is 319 entries a Bay Area birder might hold after trips to Arizona and Massachusetts, forms and a slash included. Codes are eBird's own (taxonomy 2027, fetched 2026-10-08), kept when they are a banding code or are formed from the name SnowRaven shows. Calendar and Species Detail show the species-level lists those surfaces show at their defaults.
