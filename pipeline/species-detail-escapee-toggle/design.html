A third switch: Show escapees

At the default, Species Detail now reads the same count as the Statistics headline. One press puts the three escapee-only species back in the selector, and the number with them.

01 · Today and refined

The toolbar row, before and after

Same export, same row. Today the figure reads 267 while Statistics reads 264, because this tab has no counterpart to the escapee rule. Refined, a third switch carries that rule in the tab's own verb: off by default, so the two tabs agree at their defaults, and one press restores Graylag Goose, Swan Goose and Muscovy Duck along with the number.

02 · The press

Show escapees, off and on

Off hides the three escapee-only species and reads 264; on restores them and reads 267. Switching off while an escapee is selected clears the selection, exactly as Show all forms does for a form. With Show subspecies on, a domestic-type form hides with its parent.

The list is pinned open here so the rows can be watched. The shipped picker closes on any click outside it and shows the changed list on its next open.

Refined live, list open

264 and 267 are the reference export's figures. The Show subspecies and Show all forms deltas in this page (+14 and +6) are stand-ins; the app derives them from the export.

04 · States and motion

Rest, hover, focus, on

Focus is the app's global ring. Hover is the one proposed addition to the shared switch: the border steps to the medium token and the fill to the subtle surface, 120ms ease-out. The knob slides in 180ms ease-out, the list opens in 140ms from the input's edge, and both settle in place under Reduce Motion.
