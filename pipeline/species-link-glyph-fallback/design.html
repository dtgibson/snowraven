About the favicons in this sheet. The two rasters are drawn as neutral stand-ins, not the real eBird and Birds of the World marks — nothing third-party is copied or bundled here, exactly as nothing is in the build. They are drawn at the real 14×14 full-bleed footprint and, in the dark panel, as the flat white silhouette at 0.65 opacity that the shipped [data-theme="dark"] img.sr-favicon filter actually produces. That is what the mixed pair has to sit beside, so it is what matters here.

02

Why this pair, at 14 pixels

Circle against square is the strongest difference two 14px marks can have. Both candidates draw to the edge of the box, so the pair matches the full-bleed rasters they stand in for — and matches each other. Everything below is the real lucide v1.14.0 geometry at stroke 2.2, shown at 8× with the shipped 14px beside it.

eBird
Globe

The worldwide observation network. Three strokes, all long and widely separated — the interior survives at 14px on a 1× display, which is the whole test. Ink box 20 units; outer extent 12.95px in the 14px slot.

Birds of the World
SquareLibrary

The shelf of species accounts. The frame carries the identification on its own, so the mark still reads even where the three spines merge; the leaning spine is the tell. Ink box 18 units; outer extent 11.8px — smaller than the circle by measurement, equal to it by eye.

Not used
BookOpen — proposed

This is the Species Detail tab glyph. The bird name it would sit beside is itself the button to Species Detail, so an open book here points at the wrong destination from two inches away. Overridden.

Not used
Binoculars — proposed

Already the Map Explorer's "lifers" mode glyph, and eleven strokes inside 14px collapse to a dark blob well before the meaning arrives. Overridden.

Not used
Earth

The same circle as Globe, filled with 2-unit continental jogs that are sub-pixel at 14px. It becomes a circle with grit in it.

Not used
BookOpenText

Its four text ticks are 2 units long — 1.17px at this size, so they vanish. What is left is BookOpen, inheriting BookOpen's collision.

Not used
Library

Right idea, wrong frame. Four bare spines with nothing to hold them have no silhouette: beside a solid raster favicon in the mixed pair they read as a fragment, not a mark. SquareLibrary is the same idea with a boundary.

03

The calls

Decision 1 · glyphs

Globe for eBird, SquareLibrary for Birds of the World.

The idea's Binoculars / BookOpen pair is overridden: both are spoken for in this app, and a book beside a name that already navigates to Species Detail points at the wrong place. Circle against rounded square is the cleanest 14px separation available.

Decision 2 · size & stroke

14px nominal, stroke 2.2, round caps, both marks.

The favicon's own footprint, so the slot never moves. Stroke 2.2 in a 24 viewBox renders 1.28px at 14px — the design system's in-content weight. No per-glyph size correction: the square's larger enclosed area already balances the circle.

Decision 3 · color

Set to var(--sr-text), carried by the anchor's existing 0.75 / 1 opacity step.

Set explicitly, never inherited — currentColor alone would pick up the accent inside a link context on some of the ten surfaces. No new hover rule and no dark-mode filter: --sr-text flips themes by itself.

Decision 4 · contrast

7.96:1 light, 7.63:1 dark, at rest, worst case.

Against a 3:1 floor. Both muted tokens were measured and both fail in light (--sr-text-muted 2.93:1, --sr-text-gray 2.77:1 at 0.75 opacity), so ink is the only token that clears in both themes.

Decision 5 · the mixed pair

Acceptable as drawn, because both glyphs are full-bleed.

A raster favicon fills its 14px box; so do these. The pair keeps one size, one spacing and one baseline, and reads as two marks of the same kind — which is why the bare Library was dropped.

Decision 6 · motion

None. This is a substitution, not an entrance.

Offline, onError fires on every mark in a 200-row table at unpredictable moments; any transition would be the doctrine's staggered-fade-in anti-pattern, and onLoad would need to undo it. Nothing animates, so there is no reduced-motion clause to write.

06

What this refinement does not touch

The success state is untouched: both favicons load from both hosts on exactly the schedule they do today, at the same size, in the same slots. The dark-mode img.sr-favicon filter is left alone and deliberately does not reach the glyph — brightness(0) invert(1) over a token-colored stroke would fight the token it is meant to honour.

Nothing moves in either direction: the 14px slot, the 5px gap, the 6px left margin, the 5px padding with its matching negative margin, and the resulting 24×24 targets are all as shipped. The glyph is aria-hidden, so the anchor's accessible name is the only name announced — unchanged, and still the only place the destination is spelled out.

In dark theme the glyph sits at a higher measured contrast than the silhouetted favicon beside it (9.4:1 against 5.0:1 on --sr-surface). That gap is left in place on purpose: a 1.28px outline covers roughly a quarter of the ink a filled raster does, so the two land at comparable weight to the eye, and closing the numbers would mean either dimming the fallback toward its 3:1 floor or brightening the shipped success state, which is out of scope.

No new token is minted, no new dependency is added, and no CSS in .sr-favicon-slot changes — the two guards that parse it stay green.
