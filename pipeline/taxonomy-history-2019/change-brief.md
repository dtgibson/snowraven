# Change Brief — taxonomy-history-2019

## What is changing
Splits and lumps goes from eBird's 2023 to 2025 updates back to 2019 (the 2019, 2021 and 2022 updates; eBird released none in 2020). The generator's resolvers get fixed year by year, the three announced tallies go on their `UPDATE_PUBLISHED` rows, the asset is regenerated, and the tests and input README are brought up to date.
Measured 2026-10-21 in a read-only dry run (nothing written):
- **Baseline is clean.** With only the 2022 to 2025 inputs, today's generator reproduces the committed asset byte for byte (93,932 bytes, `generated` 2026-09-30).
- **2019** derives 107 gained and 22 lost against the announced 106 and 20: one split and two lumps too many.
- **2021** stops at `split daughter "Oxapampa Antpitta" (oxaant1) has 2 parent candidates (rufant1, cheant2)`, with no taxonomy or previous-sort link.
- **2022 also fails, which the handoff did not know.** Run alone (2021 to 2025 inputs), it stops at `split of "Brace's Emerald" (braeme1) has 1 daughter species (braeme2)`. Announced: 118 / 41.
Prefer a general rule to a per-species fix. If a case really needs a named override, write it as a cited table like `UPDATE_PUBLISHED`, give its source, and keep it under the tally check. The tally check stays required and is not weakened. Write each decision at the point where it is implemented and in `decisions.md`.

## Why now
ROADMAP's "Open from big-old-life-lists" item (1) was blocked only on inputs. On 2026-10-21 all 14 Cornell files arrived, along with the three tallies (sources are in `originals/SOURCES.md`). Since 1.0.57, very large and very old exports load. Those exports carry names that predate 2023, and the section cannot account for them yet.
