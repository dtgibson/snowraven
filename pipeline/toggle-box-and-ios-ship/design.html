What changes, exactly

  • The box goes away. The bordered button chrome existed to wrap a visible label next to the switch. Settings hides that label, so the empty frame is all that remained. The switch itself becomes the control.
  • The bare switch grows slightly (36×20 track, 16px knob, up from 28×16 / 12px) so it holds its own beside the row text without the frame. Same tokens, same 180ms ease-out slide. Every other switch in the app keeps the boxed treatment and is untouched.
  • Keyboard and touch are preserved. The app's global focus ring draws around the bare switch, and invisible padding keeps the tap area comfortable on a phone.
