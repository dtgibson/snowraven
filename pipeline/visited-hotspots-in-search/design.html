What is new, and only this

Everything else in the overlay is the shipped one, rule for rule.

  • A third group, LocationsUnder Species, in the same list. The arrow keys run straight through all three groups and Enter opens the highlighted row.
  • A place reads as a placeThe name on one line, the county and state beneath it. Long names wrap in full, because eBird names differ at the end.
  • Namesakes stay apartSame name in different counties: the county tells them apart. Same name and same county: each row adds its Location ID.
  • Each cut is said where it happensWhen a group stops at 50, its line sits under that group's last row, so two lines can show at once without confusion.
  • One sentence about your backupSpecies and locations come from one file, so one sentence speaks for both.
  • A short hint on a narrow fieldThe full wording stays where it fits. On a phone the field reads Search.
