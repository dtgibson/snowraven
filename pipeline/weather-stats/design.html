2 · The same section at 320px

The hardest part of this layout, so it is shown rather than promised. Every row keeps its full label — nothing is ellipsized to "Scatte…" — because at the phone tier the bar drops to its own line beneath the name and count. That is the shipped .sr-proj-row stacking move. Try the 200% Text size button with this frame in view: the names wrap, the bars keep their full width, and nothing forces the page sideways.

320px — fixed, ignores the Width control
What the 320px rules do

Distribution rows go from glyph | name | bar | count to two lines: glyph | name | count over a full-width bar. The name gets white-space: normal so it wraps instead of truncating.

Band blocks (species and time out) drop their figure under its own bar, so each bar still sits directly above the number it draws.

The axis toggle goes full width and takes the 2.75rem touch posture, matching every other phone-tier control in the app.

The species picker takes the full column width; its sm 220px cap is lifted only at the phone tier.

No positive min-width anywhere in the section, so it cannot leak horizontal page scroll at 320px and 200% text.
