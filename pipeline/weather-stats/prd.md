# PRD — Weather Stats

**Feature:** weather-stats
**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Stage:** 2 — The Planner
**Source:** strategic-brief.md (approved)

---

## Feature Overview

A **Weather** section on the Statistics tab that reads the weather blocks
SnowRaven and RainCrow have been writing into the user's checklist comments,
and reports how their birding distributes across sky condition, temperature,
wind, and day versus night, plus what they recorded in each. Everything is
derived from the eBird backup already loaded: no network call, no backend
change, no write of any kind, and no change to `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.

---

## User Stories

> **US-01** — As a birder opening the Weather section, I want the fraction of my
> checklists these figures actually describe stated before any chart, so that I
> never read a number without knowing how much of my birding is behind it.

> **US-02** — As a birder, I want to see how my outings distribute across sky
> condition, temperature band, wind band, and day versus night, so that I can
> answer "what weather do I actually go out in" from a record rather than a
> memory.

> **US-03** — As a birder, I want average species per checklist by temperature
> band and by condition with the same bands' average time out shown beside it,
> so that I can see whether a lower count is the birds or is me going home
> early.

> **US-04** — As a birder curious about one bird, I want to pick a species and
> see the conditions and temperature bands I have recorded it in with counts,
> so that I get a record of what happened rather than a claim about the bird.

> **US-05** — As a birder with few or no weather blocks, I want a plain
> statement of that and a route to the Weather Backlog, so that I am never shown
> an empty chart or an average computed from three checklists.

> **US-06** — As a birder whose export contains a block this app cannot fully
> read, I want the fields that did read to still count and the number of wholly
> unreadable blocks shown, so that a parser quietly breaking looks like a number
> rather than a smaller chart.

> **US-07** — As a birder who came to SnowRaven from raincrow.app, I want my
> older RainCrow blocks counted, so that switching apps does not delete half my
> weather history.

## Functional Requirements

### A. The block parser

> **FR-01** — The app shall locate a weather block by its SPAN, using a span
> finder exported from `lib/commentBlocks.ts` and shared with
> `stripWeatherTideBlocks`. There shall be exactly one span implementation in
> the codebase. `commentBlocks.ts` shall export a function returning every block
> span in a comment as `{ start, end, kind }` where `kind` is `'weather'`,
> `'tide'`, or `'combined'`, decided by which attribution alternative matched
> (`weather and tide generated by` → combined, `weather generated by` →
> weather, `tide data from noaa co-ops` → tide), and `stripWeatherTideBlocks`
> shall be refactored to build its output from that same function.

> **FR-02** — The parser shall operate on the entity-DECODED comment
> (`decodeEntities` from `lib/commentText.ts`) and shall never touch
> `innerHTML`. Span offsets are indices into the decoded string.

> **FR-03** — The parser shall NOT be line-based. eBird's CSV export collapses
> the pasted block's newlines into spaces, so within a span a labeled value is
> terminated by the START OF THE NEXT KNOWN LABEL, or by the start of the
> attribution, or by the end of the span, whichever comes first. It is never
> terminated by a newline or by end-of-line.

> **FR-04** — The parser shall read the condition from the leading emoji run of
> the span, matched against the closed 11-value set emitted by `conditionEmoji`
> in `lib/weatherFormatter.ts`: `⛈️ 🌦️ 🌧️ ❄️ 🌫️ ☀️ 🌤️ ⛅ 🌥️ ☁️ 🌡️`. Matching
> shall be insensitive to the U+FE0F variation selector: ten of the eleven
> glyphs carry it and `⛅` (U+26C5) does not, and a round-trip through eBird or a
> user's clipboard can add or drop it. The condition shall never be grouped on
> the block's free-text description line.

> **FR-05** — The parser shall report a checklist as NIGHT when the header emoji
> run contains one of the eight moon-phase glyphs `🌑 🌒 🌓 🌔 🌕 🌖 🌗 🌘`
> (which carry no variation selector), and as DAY when the run carries a
> condition glyph and no moon glyph. When neither is present the day/night field
> is missing, not defaulted.

> **FR-06** — The parser shall read `Temperature:` as a RANGE. The value is
> either `52 - 58°F` or, when the sampled hours agreed, a single `55°F`; a
> single value parses as `low = high`. A temperature shall be rejected as a
> missing field when either endpoint falls outside −80 to 140 °F inclusive, or
> when `low > high`. A rejected field does not make the block unreadable.

> **FR-07** — The parser shall read `Wind:` as an ordered run of one or more
> Beaufort words joined by ` - `, drawn from the closed 9-value set in
> `weatherFormatter.ts`: Calm, Mostly calm, Light breeze, Gentle breeze,
> Moderate breeze, Fresh breeze, Strong breeze, Near gale, Gale. The formatter
> emits a de-duplicated, Beaufort-sorted set, so three or more labels are
> possible and the parser shall not assume exactly two. The app shall NOT invert
> the mph threshold table to synthesize a wind speed. An unrecognized word makes
> the wind field missing.

> **FR-08** — The parser shall read `Cloud Cover:` as a percent range with the
> same range grammar as FR-06, rejecting any endpoint outside 0 to 100 inclusive
> or a range where `low > high`.

> **FR-09** — The parser shall also read `Wind Direction:`, `Humidity:`,
> `Dew point:`, `Sunrise:` and `Sunset:` into the record, so a follow-on run
> needs no parser change. These fields are not charted in v1.

> **FR-10** — The parser shall return a PARTIAL record: each field is present or
> absent independently, and a block that yields a temperature but not a wind
> contributes to the temperature figures and to no others. The app shall never
> discard a whole block because one field failed.

> **FR-11** — Where a comment contains more than one weather span (a block
> pasted twice, or a weather block and a separate combined block), the app shall
> use the FIRST weather-or-combined span and ignore the rest. A tide span is
> never parsed for weather.

> **FR-12** — The parser shall be pure and read-only: it takes a string, returns
> a record, writes nothing, normalizes nothing, and never modifies a comment.
> The comment in the user's eBird account is the source of truth.

### B. Coverage and gating

> **FR-13** — The section's denominator shall be the ATTRIBUTION-based pair,
> `hasSnowravenWeatherBlock(comment) || hasRaincrowWeatherBlock(comment)` from
> `lib/commentBlocks.ts`, which is exactly the definition behind Data Quality's
> "Any weather" bar. A comment carrying weather LABELS but no app attribution
> shall count as no block at all, in neither the found nor the unreadable
> count.

> **FR-14** — The section shall open with a coverage line stating the number of
> checklists carrying a readable weather block, that number as a percentage of
> the user's total checklists, and it shall appear above every chart. Copy:
> "These figures cover the N checklists that carry a weather block SnowRaven or
> RainCrow wrote. That is X% of your M checklists."

> **FR-15** — Where the number of blocks found but wholly unreadable is greater
> than zero, the coverage line shall state it: "K more carry a block this app
> could not read." A block is wholly unreadable when it was found by FR-13 and
> the parser returned no field at all.

> **FR-16** — Where the found count is zero, the section shall not render and
> its jump-nav entry shall not appear. This follows the shipped Data Quality
> weather bars, which render only when a block exists, and the shipped
> conditional `Media` nav entry.

> **FR-17** — Where the readable count is greater than zero but below
> `WEATHER_SECTION_MIN_READABLE`, the section shall render the coverage line and
> a pointer to the Weather Backlog, and nothing else. No chart, no average, no
> per-species picker. Copy: "You have N checklists with a readable weather
> block. That is not enough to chart yet." followed by a link reading "Fill in
> the gaps from the Weather Backlog."

> **FR-18** — Both thresholds shall be named constants in `lib/weatherStats.ts`,
> each carrying a one-line reason in the code, following the shipped
> `RATINGS_MIN_TO_SHOW` / `ONLY_ADULTS_MIN_ASSETS` pattern:
> `WEATHER_SECTION_MIN_READABLE = 5` (a distribution across eleven conditions
> built on four checklists is four checklists wearing a chart) and
> `WEATHER_BAND_MIN_TO_SHOW = 8` (below this a band's average reads as noise).
> `WEATHER_BAND_MIN_TO_SHOW` deliberately carries the same value as
> `RATINGS_MIN_TO_SHOW` and shall NOT import it: the two are independent
> judgments that happen to agree.

### C. Banding

> **FR-19** — A temperature shall be assigned to exactly ONE band, by its
> MIDPOINT: `(low + high) / 2`, rounded to a whole degree with the repo's
> existing `bankersRound` so the half-degree tie rule is inherited rather than
> invented, then placed in the integer band containing it. The seven bands are:
> Below 32°F, 32 to 44°F, 45 to 54°F, 55 to 64°F, 65 to 74°F, 75 to 84°F, and
> 85°F and up. The app shall NOT count a checklist in every band its range
> overlaps.

> **FR-20** — A wind range shall be assigned to exactly ONE band, by the
> ORDINAL midpoint of its Beaufort indices: `floor((minIndex + maxIndex) / 2)`.
> A tie resolves DOWNWARD, toward the calmer band, because the formatter emits a
> de-duplicated set in which one gusty hour contributes a whole extra label with
> the same weight as an hour that lasted the outing, making the high end of a
> run the least representative end.

> **FR-21** — Every distribution figure shall sum to the count of readable
> checklists carrying that field. Each axis states its own denominator, because
> a checklist can have a temperature and no wind.

> **FR-22** — The day/night split shall come from FR-05 and shall be shown as
> two counts against the readable checklists that carried a header emoji run.

### D. The figures

> **FR-23** — The section shall render a distribution of readable checklists by
> condition emoji, by temperature band, by wind band, and by day versus night.
> This is descriptive, so it renders at any sample size at or above FR-17's
> floor, including for bands with a count of zero, which shall be shown as zero
> rather than dropped: dropping a band makes the distribution itself lie.

> **FR-24** — The section shall render average species recorded per checklist by
> temperature band and by condition, computed from `ChecklistEntry.speciesCount`
> so it follows the same "Count spuh and slash" rule as the rest of the
> Statistics tab.

> **FR-25** — Every species-per-band figure shall render the same bands' average
> checklist duration beside it. The duration average carries its OWN denominator
> (checklists in the band with a non-null `duration`), which may be smaller than
> the band's checklist count, and that denominator shall be visible in the
> figure.

> **FR-26** — A band with fewer than `WEATHER_BAND_MIN_TO_SHOW` checklists shall
> show its checklist count and NO derived average, for both the species figure
> and the duration figure. The band shall still appear.

> **FR-27** — The section shall carry the effort confound in one sentence, once,
> near the species-per-band figures. Copy: "Weather changes how long you stay
> out, so a lower species count can be the weather or it can be the outing.
> Average time out sits beside every average so you can see both."

> **FR-28** — The section shall offer a per-species view: pick a species, see
> the conditions and temperature bands that species was recorded in, with
> counts. The picker shall be the shipped `components/SpeciesCombobox.tsx`, not
> a new one. Its options shall be limited to species recorded on at least one
> readable-block checklist. Counts are CHECKLISTS the species appears on that
> carry the relevant readable field, never observation rows.

> **FR-29** — No copy in the section shall predict, recommend, or rank. The
> phrases "best conditions for", "you should try", "expect to find", and the
> word "correlation" shall not appear, and no leaderboard of species by weather
> shall be rendered.

### E. Integration

> **FR-30** — The section shall ship as its own component file,
> `frontend/src/components/WeatherStatsSection.tsx`, following the shipped
> heavy-section precedent (`MediaStatsSections.tsx`, `ProjectsSection.tsx`,
> `FrivolousListsSections.tsx`). `BirdingStats.tsx` is 2,326 lines and shall not
> grow by a section's worth.

> **FR-31** — The section shall be one `SectionCard` from
> `components/statsPrimitives.tsx` titled "Weather", placed immediately after
> "Data Quality", with a matching conditional jump-nav entry built the way the
> shipped `Media` entry is.

> **FR-32** — The derivation shall join the existing worker bundle:
> `computeWeatherStats(checklists)` and the per-species tally shall be called
> from `computeStatsBundle` in `lib/statsBundle.ts`, and the new field shall be
> added to BOTH the `StatsBundle` interface AND the `BUNDLE_FIELDS`
> `Record<keyof StatsBundle, true>` table, so omitting the second is a build
> error rather than a hole in `isStatsBundle`. No deep validation of the new
> field is added; the check stays structural, matching the shipped posture.

> **FR-33** — The parser and the aggregation shall be pure and
> structured-clone-safe in both directions, so the same chain runs in
> `statsWorker.ts` and in the main-thread fallback with no second copy of the
> wiring. No function, class instance, or DOM value shall enter the bundle.

> **FR-34** — Every existing Statistics figure shall be unchanged after this
> work, and `stripWeatherTideBlocks` shall return byte-identical output for its
> whole existing fixture corpus after the FR-01 refactor. Per
> `.claude/rules/weather-tide.md`, the strip shall additionally be re-verified
> against a real export, because all three shipped strip bugs were invisible to
> formatter-fixture tests.

> **FR-35** — The change shall carry its documentation and release sweep in the
> same commit set: the Statistics part of `docs/HELP.md` gains a "Weather"
> subsection, `README.md` and `website/` state the feature, `CHANGELOG.md` gains
> its entry, `.claude/rules/weather-tide.md` gains the new parser module in its
> `paths` frontmatter and names the READER explicitly in its marker-vocabulary
> warning, and the four-file version set is bumped together
> (`frontend/package.json`, `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and
> `website/index.html`'s version pill text, its `aria-label`, and the
> `footer-version` line).

## Non-Functional Requirements

> **NFR-01 — Performance, worker budget:** the added derivation shall add no
> more than 20 ms to `computeStatsBundle` at the reference export scale (21,856
> rows, 7.18 Mchar), measured as the median of five runs on a quiet machine with
> nothing else compiling, and the measurement shall be recorded in a comment at
> the definition site alongside the existing measurements. If the measured
> addition exceeds that, `STATS_BUDGET_MS_PER_ROW`, `STATS_BUDGET_FLOOR_MS` and
> `STATS_BUDGET_CEILING_MS` shall be RE-MEASURED and re-derived, never adjusted
> by guess, and never left stale.

> **NFR-02 — Performance, reply payload:** the new bundle field shall be
> bounded by the band and condition counts and the species count, not by the row
> count, so the reply clone leg measured at 0.05 to 0.44 µs/row is not made a
> function of the export size. The per-species tally shall hold only species
> with at least one readable-block checklist.

> **NFR-03 — Security, linearity over untrusted text:** the parser scans
> untrusted export text on a thread that may be the main thread (the worker
> fallback), so every scan shall be linear by construction. No `Array.includes`,
> `indexOf` or `find` inside a loop over export text, no `filter` inside a
> `map`, no nested scan keyed on user file content; the condition set, the moon
> set and the Beaufort set shall be `Set` or `Map` lookups. Any regex shall have
> its lazy quantifiers length-bounded and its `lastIndex` reset before reuse.
> Linearity shall be proved by timing the exported entry point at 10k / 20k /
> 40k hostile input sizes and requiring roughly 2x growth per doubling, with the
> hostile input built so the pattern FAILS after consuming the run (a near-miss
> suffix), never a fixture where the match succeeds at the first start position.

> **NFR-04 — Accessibility:** every `<button>` and every `<a href>` the section
> renders shall carry a literal `tabIndex={0}` (WebKit's default tab mode, which
> the shipped Mac, iPhone and iPad apps run, skips both without it, and
> `lib/tabOrderCoverage.test.ts` asserts it on every build). Every color shall
> come from a `var(--sr-*)` token in both themes, with no hardcoded hex or RGB.
> WCAG 2.1 AA shall hold at 320px width and 200% in-app text scale. Every chart
> shall carry `role="img"` and an `aria-label` naming its values, following the
> shipped `Donut` in `MediaStatsSections.tsx`. Reusing `SpeciesCombobox` (FR-28)
> satisfies the listbox and `aria-activedescendant` shape.

> **NFR-05 — DOM identifiers:** any `id` or ARIA IDREF the section creates shall
> key on an INDEX, never on a species name, a condition emoji, a band label, or
> any other user or data value. An id built from a data value can carry
> whitespace, which cannot resolve as an IDREF and silently switches off the
> announcement it exists for.

> **NFR-06 — Copy:** no em dash (U+2014) in any user-facing string, per
> `.claude/rules/docs-and-website.md`. A published claim states the property,
> never a count. User-facing surfaces are named from `TAB_LABELS` in
> `lib/tabLayout.ts`, never from a component name.

> **NFR-07 — Privacy and scope:** frontend-only. No network call, no new
> provider, no backend change, no write to storage, no telemetry, and no change
> to `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`. The Python formatter is untouched.

> **NFR-08 — Offline:** the section shall be fully functional with no network,
> because every figure derives from the loaded backup.

## Out of Scope

- A "birds most associated with rain" leaderboard, or any ranked claim about
  species by weather. The per-species view (FR-28) is the v1 answer.
- Predictive or recommending copy of any kind.
- Statistical inference: no p-values, no significance tests, no confidence
  intervals, and no use of the word "correlation" in user copy.
- Tide blocks. Same comments, own run, because a tide state is a moment rather
  than a range and it doubles the parse surface.
- Moon phase as an axis. It is in the block, but only on night checklists.
- Humidity, dew point, wind direction, and cloud cover as CHARTED axes. All four
  are parsed and banded so the next run needs no parser change; see OQ-02 for the
  cloud cover reasoning.
- Cross-checklist weather lookups. A checklist with no block has no weather, and
  the app does not fetch one to fill a gap.
- Any write. The parser never modifies a comment, and the feature never offers to
  add a block to one. Bulk backfill is a different feature.
- A new tab, and any change to the tab navigation.
- Surfacing weather on Species Detail, the Calendar, or the Map Explorer.
- Unit conversion. Blocks are written in °F and percent and the figures read in
  the units the blocks carry.
- Backend work of any kind.
- Changing the Weather Backlog's own denominator to match this section's. The two
  differ deliberately; see FR-13 and QA-08.

## Open Questions

**OQ-01 — At zero found blocks, is the section absent or does it render a plain
statement?** The brief's Success Criteria says a user with no weather blocks
"sees a plain statement and a route to the Weather Backlog"; its Key Decisions
says "a section can be absent, the way the Data Quality weather bars already
are". These pull opposite ways at exactly zero.
*Default if unanswered:* absent (FR-16). The operative half of the success
criterion is "never an empty chart or a figure computed from nothing", which an
absent section satisfies maximally, and the shipped precedent one card up is
absence. The stated cost is that a user with zero blocks gets no route to the
backlog from Statistics; the Weather tab remains their discovery surface, and
that is no worse than today.

**OQ-02 — Is cloud cover charted?** The brief's field table gives cloud cover a
v1 use of "banded", but its distribution scope lists only condition, temperature,
wind, and day/night.
*Default if unanswered:* parsed and banded, NOT charted. Four of the eleven
condition emoji already ARE cloud tiers (clear, few, scattered, broken,
overcast), so a cloud-cover percent chart beside the condition chart is two views
of one axis. The band function ships and is tested, so charting it later is a
component change with no parser change.

**OQ-03 — Is `WEATHER_SECTION_MIN_READABLE = 5` the right floor?** It is a
judgment, not a measurement.
*Default if unanswered:* 5, with its reason in the code. Raising it hides the
section from more real users; lowering it draws a distribution across eleven
conditions from four checklists.

**OQ-04 — Does the section report its median temperature span width?** The
brief offers this as the honest disclosure of the midpoint decision (FR-19):
"if it matters, report the median span width once rather than fixing it per
figure."
*Default if unanswered:* yes, one sentence in the coverage area, e.g. "Your
blocks cover a median range of 6°F, and each checklist is counted once, at the
middle of its range." It costs one number and it is the stated mitigation for
the single decision most able to mislead.

**OQ-05 — Does the Weather Backlog gain a reciprocal pointer back to this
section?** The brief's Key Decisions describes the loop as "the Statistics
section points at the backlog when coverage is thin, and the backlog points at
the section."
*Default if unanswered:* yes, one line of copy on the Weather Backlog, using the
existing in-app navigation and adding no new machinery.
